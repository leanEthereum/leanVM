\section{Proving Keccak by GKR}\label{annex:keccak}

\subsection{Credits}\label{keccak:credits}

The design is based on Lev Soukhanov's Hashcaster~2~\cite{Hashcaster2}. The packing is a reverse multiplication friendly embedding~\cite{CCXY18}.

\subsection{Overview}\label{keccak:overview}

The prover commits the permutations' inputs; the main protocol ends with claims on their outputs. A round of Keccak-$f$ is a linear map followed by chi, of degree two. GKR~\cite{GKR08} goes back one round at a time: a \emph{layer} turns a claim on a round's output into a claim on its input.

\subsection{Keccak-\texorpdfstring{$f[1600]$}{f[1600]}}\label{keccak:perm}

A state is $1600$ bits: $25$ lanes of $64$ bits, lane $\ell$ in row $\lfloor\ell/5\rfloor$ and column $\ell\bmod5$ of a $5\times5$ grid. Position $j=z+64\ell$ is bit $z$ (\emph{slice} $z$) of lane $\ell$.

For a lane $0\le\ell<25$ and $0\le k<5$, $\along{\ell}{k}$ is the lane in the same row as $\ell$ whose column is that of $\ell$ plus $k$, modulo $5$, and $\along{j}{k}$ is the same slice of that lane:
\[
\along{\ell}{k}=5\lfloor\ell/5\rfloor+\bigl((\ell\bmod5)+k\bigr)\bmod5,\qquad \along{(z+64\ell)}{k}=z+64\,\along{\ell}{k}.
\]
For example $\along{3}{1}=4$ and $\along{4}{1}=0$. The map $j\mapsto\along{j}{k}$ is a bijection with inverse $j\mapsto\along{j}{5-k}$. Chi uses $k=1,2$.

Round $R\in\{1,\dots,24\}$ maps a state $a$ to
\begin{equation}\label{keccak:eq:round}
b=\klin(a),\qquad a'[j]=b[j]+\bigl(1+b[\along{j}{1}]\bigr)\,b[\along{j}{2}]+[\,j<64\,]\,\krc_R[j],
\end{equation}
where $\klin$ is pi $\circ$ rho $\circ$ theta, the product is chi, and $\krc_R$ is FIPS~202's $\mathrm{RC}[R-1]$, added by iota.

\subsection{Packing}\label{keccak:rmfe}

\begin{lemma}[Embedding]\label{lem:rmfe}
There are $\Ftwo$-linear maps $\kemb:\Ftwo^{16}\to\Ftwo^{\le23}[X]$ and $\kproj:\Ftwo^{\le46}[X]\to\Ftwo^{16}$ with $\kproj\bigl(\kemb(u)\cdot\kemb(v)\bigr)=u\wedge v$ for all $u,v$, $\wedge$ being the coordinatewise product.
\end{lemma}

The proof is in \S\ref{keccak:rmfe-proof}. Let $\kemb_i$ embed the unit vector at $i\in\cube{4}$ and $\kemb_{\allone}=\sum_i\kemb_i$, so that $\kproj(\kemb_{\allone}\cdot\kemb(u))=u$. Each bit of $\kproj(G)$ is a sum of coefficients of $G$, so $\kproj$ applies just as well to a polynomial $G$ with coefficients in $\E$, giving $\kproj(G)\in\E^{16}$. For weights $\kw\in\E^{16}$, write $\kproj_{\kw}(G)=\sum_i\kw_i\,\kproj(G)_i$. 

The key idea is to \emph{pack} the permutations in groups of sixteen. At one position, the bits of a pack's sixteen permutations form a vector $u\in\Ftwo^{16}$, which $\kemb$ turns into a single polynomial. Chi's product of two bits then becomes, for the whole pack, one carry-less product of two such polynomials, from which $\kproj$ reads back the sixteen products.

\subsection{Example: chi on one layer}\label{keccak:idea}

Take $2^{\kbatch}$ permutations in \emph{packs} of sixteen: permutation $t=(i,p)$ is number $i\in\cube{4}$ of pack $p\in\cube{\kbatch-4}$. Keep only chi; the linear map and the round constant are left to \S\ref{keccak:layer}. With $b(j;t)$ the bits entering chi, the bits leaving it are
\[
a'(j;t)=b(j;t)+\bigl(1+b(\along{j}{1};t)\bigr)\,b(\along{j}{2};t),
\]
The verifier holds a claim on a weighted sum of them, with weights $\eq(r,p)$ on the packs ($r\in\E^{\kbatch-4}$), $\kw_i$ within a pack and $\kpw_j$ on the positions:
\[
c=\sum_{p\in\cube{\kbatch-4}}\eq(r,p)\sum_{i\in\cube{4}}\kw_i\sum_{j<1600}\kpw_j\,a'\bigl(j;(i,p)\bigr).
\]

\paragraph{1. The product polynomial.} In pack $p$ at position $j$, let $S_{p,j}=\kemb\bigl((b(j;(i,p)))_{i\in\cube{4}}\bigr)$. Since $\kproj(\kemb_{\allone}\kemb(u))=u$ and $\kproj(\kemb(u)\kemb(v))=u\wedge v$, the polynomial $G_{p,j}=\kemb_{\allone}S_{p,j}+(\kemb_{\allone}+S_{p,\along{j}{1}})\,S_{p,\along{j}{2}}$ projects to the sixteen outputs of pack $p$ at $j$. The prover sends the $47$ coefficients of
\[
G=\sum_{p\in\cube{\kbatch-4}}\eq(r,p)\sum_{j<1600}\kpw_j\,G_{p,j},
\]
and the verifier checks $\kproj_{\kw}(G)=c$.

\paragraph{2. One state per pack.} The verifier tests $G$ at a random point: it samples $\kpt\in\E$ and computes $G(\kpt)$ from the coefficients it received. A wrong $G$ has the wrong value there, except with probability $46/|\E|$, so it remains to prove what $G(\kpt)$ should be.

Evaluating at $\kpt$ respects sums and products, and $\kemb$ is linear, so $S_{p,j}(\kpt)$ is a fixed combination of the sixteen bits of pack $p$ at position $j$:
\[
S_{p,j}(\kpt)=\Bigl(\sum_{i\in\cube{4}}b\bigl(j;(i,p)\bigr)\,\kemb_i\Bigr)(\kpt)=\sum_{i\in\cube{4}}\kw'_i\,b\bigl(j;(i,p)\bigr),\qquad \kw'_i=\kemb_i(\kpt).
\]
For each pack, the $1600$ values $S_{p,j}(\kpt)$ thus form one state over $\E$, a random combination of the pack's sixteen states. With $\kwsum'=\kemb_{\allone}(\kpt)=\sum_{i\in\cube{4}}\kw'_i$, the value of $G$ is chi applied to these states:
\begin{align*}
G(\kpt)&=\sum_{p\in\cube{\kbatch-4}}\eq(r,p)\sum_{j<1600}\kpw_j\,G_{p,j}(\kpt)\\
&=\sum_{p\in\cube{\kbatch-4}}\eq(r,p)\sum_{j<1600}\kpw_j\Bigl(\kemb_{\allone}(\kpt)\,S_{p,j}(\kpt)+\bigl(\kemb_{\allone}(\kpt)+S_{p,\along{j}{1}}(\kpt)\bigr)\,S_{p,\along{j}{2}}(\kpt)\Bigr)\\
&=\sum_{p\in\cube{\kbatch-4}}\eq(r,p)\sum_{j<1600}\kpw_j\Bigl(\kwsum'\,S_{p,j}(\kpt)+\bigl(\kwsum'+S_{p,\along{j}{1}}(\kpt)\bigr)\,S_{p,\along{j}{2}}(\kpt)\Bigr).
\end{align*}

\paragraph{3. Sumcheck.} The right side is a sum over packs and positions (positions past $1600$ count as zero). A sumcheck runs over it, first on the $\kbatch-4$ bits of $p$, then on the $11$ bits of $j$, and ends at a random pack $\fc^{\mathrm{pack}}\in\E^{\kbatch-4}$ and a random position $\fc^{\mathrm{pos}}\in\E^{11}$, where the summand is
\[
\eq(r,\fc^{\mathrm{pack}})\;\mle{\kpw}(\fc^{\mathrm{pos}})\;\bigl(\kwsum'\,\kev_0+(\kwsum'+\kev_1)\,\kev_2\bigr),\qquad
\kev_k=\sum_{j<1600}\eq(\fc^{\mathrm{pos}},j)\,S_{\fc^{\mathrm{pack}},\along{j}{k}}(\kpt),
\]
with $S_{\fc,j}=\sum_{p\in\cube{\kbatch-4}}\eq(\fc,p)\,S_{p,j}$ the extension of $S$ in $p$: $\kev_k$ is the state at the random pack, shifted by $k$ along the rows, at the random position. The prover sends $\kev_0,\kev_1,\kev_2$; the verifier computes $\eq(r,\fc^{\mathrm{pack}})$ and $\mle{\kpw}(\fc^{\mathrm{pos}})$ and checks the summand against the sumcheck's last claim.

\paragraph{4. One claim again.} The verifier samples $\kbeta$ and sets $c'=\kev_0+\kbeta\,\kev_1+\kbeta^2\,\kev_2$. Each $\kev_k$ is a weighted sum of the bits entering chi, so
\[
c'=\sum_{p\in\cube{\kbatch-4}}\eq(\fc^{\mathrm{pack}},p)\sum_{i\in\cube{4}}\kw'_i\sum_{j<1600}\kpw'_j\,b\bigl(j;(i,p)\bigr),
\]
with $\kpw'_j=\eq(\fc^{\mathrm{pos}},j)+\kbeta\,\eq(\fc^{\mathrm{pos}},\along{j}{4})+\kbeta^{2}\,\eq(\fc^{\mathrm{pos}},\along{j}{3})$, the shifts moved onto the weights: a claim of the same form as $c$, on the bits entering chi.

\subsection{Statement and commitment}\label{keccak:statement}

A table row is a \emph{permutation}: it reads a state from memory, its \emph{input}, and applies Keccak-$f[1600]$. The table has $2^{\kbatch}$ rows, $\kbatch\ge9$ (\S\ref{sec:arith}), indexed by $t\in\cube{\kbatch}$, low bit first, and written $t=(i,p)$ with $i\in\cube{4}$ the low four bits and $p\in\cube{\kbatch-4}$ the \emph{pack}; padding rows hold the instruction applied to the zero state (\S\ref{sec:e2e-pad}).

The prover commits the inputs as $\qkeccak:\cube{\kbatch}\times\cube{5}\to\K$: slot $\ell<25$ holds input lane $\ell$, bit $z$ being the coefficient of $x^z$, and the other slots hold zero. Its bit slices $Q_z$ satisfy $\qkeccak=\sum_{z<64}Q_z\,x^z$ (Annex~\ref{annex:rs}), so bit $z$ of input lane $\ell$ of permutation $t$ is $Q_z(t,\ell)$. The bus (\S\ref{sec:tab-keccak}) binds $\qkeccak$ to the state read from memory.

Let $a_e(j;t)$ be bit $j$ after $e$ rounds \eqref{keccak:eq:round} of permutation $t$, from its committed input, and $\kout{\ell}(t)=\sum_{z<64}a_{24}(z+64\ell;t)\,x^z$ its output lane $\ell$, a virtual column of the table. The table sumcheck (\S\ref{sec:e2e-unrolled}) leaves values $\koutv{\ell}$, claimed to be $\mle{\kout{\ell}}$ at one point $(r_{\mathrm{low}},r_{\mathrm{pack}})\in\E^{4}\times\E^{\kbatch-4}$. The reduction proves
\begin{equation}\label{keccak:eq:statement}
\koutv{\ell}=\mle{\kout{\ell}}(r_{\mathrm{low}},r_{\mathrm{pack}})\quad\text{for all }\ell<25.
\end{equation}

\subsection{Claims}\label{keccak:claims}

\begin{definition}[Claim]\label{def:keccak-claim}
A \emph{claim at depth $d\le24$} is $(c,\kw,r,\kpw)$ with $c\in\E$, \emph{in-pack weights} $\kw\in\E^{16}$, $r\in\E^{\kbatch-4}$ and \emph{position weights} $\kpw\in\E^{1600}$. It asserts
\begin{equation}\label{keccak:eq:claim}
c=\sum_{p\in\cube{\kbatch-4}}\ \sum_{i\in\cube{4}}\ \sum_{j<1600}\eq(r,p)\,\kw_i\,\kpw_j\;a_{24-d}\bigl(j;(i,p)\bigr).
\end{equation}
\end{definition}

\paragraph{The first claim.} After the $\koutv{\ell}$, the verifier samples $r_{\mathrm{lane}}\in\E^{5}$ and sets, at depth $0$,
\[
\kw_i=\eq(r_{\mathrm{low}},i),\qquad r=r_{\mathrm{pack}},\qquad \kpw_{z+64\ell}=\eq(r_{\mathrm{lane}},\ell)\,x^{z},\qquad c=\sum_{\ell<25}\eq(r_{\mathrm{lane}},\ell)\,\koutv{\ell}.
\]
By Fact~\ref{fact:eq}, the right side of \eqref{keccak:eq:claim} is then $\sum_\ell\eq(r_{\mathrm{lane}},\ell)\,\mle{\kout{\ell}}(r_{\mathrm{low}},r_{\mathrm{pack}})$: the first claim is a random combination of the output claims.

\subsection{A layer}\label{keccak:layer}

Layer $d<24$ takes a claim $(c,\kw,r,\kpw)$ at depth $d$ to one at depth $d+1$, through round $24-d$. With $b=\klin(a_{23-d})$,
\begin{equation}\label{keccak:eq:chi}
a_{24-d}(j;t)=b(j;t)+\bigl(1+b(\along{j}{1};t)\bigr)\,b(\along{j}{2};t)+[\,j<64\,]\,\krc_{24-d}[j].
\end{equation}

Steps 1 to 5 are \S\ref{keccak:idea} with the round constant; step 6 crosses the linear map.

\paragraph{1. Product polynomial.} Let $S_{p,j}=\kemb\bigl((b(j;(i,p)))_{i\in\cube{4}}\bigr)$ and $G_{p,j}=\kemb_{\allone}S_{p,j}+(\kemb_{\allone}+S_{p,\along{j}{1}})\,S_{p,\along{j}{2}}$; by Lemma~\ref{lem:rmfe}, $\kproj(G_{p,j})$ is pack $p$ of \eqref{keccak:eq:chi} at $j$, less the round constant. The prover sends the $47$ coefficients of
\begin{equation}\label{keccak:eq:product}
G(X)=\sum_{p\in\cube{\kbatch-4}}\ \sum_{j<1600}\eq(r,p)\,\kpw_j\,G_{p,j}(X),
\end{equation}
and, with $\kwsum=\sum_{i\in\cube{4}}\kw_i$ and $\sum_{p\in\cube{\kbatch-4}}\eq(r,p)=1$, the verifier checks
\begin{equation}\label{keccak:eq:projection}
\kproj_{\kw}(G)+\kwsum\sum_{z<64}\krc_{24-d}[z]\,\kpw_z\ \qeq\ c.
\end{equation}

\paragraph{2. Evaluation.} The verifier samples $\kpt\in\E$. Let $\kw'_i=\kemb_i(\kpt)$, $\kwsum'=\sum_{i\in\cube{4}}\kw'_i$, so that $S_{p,j}(\kpt)=\sum_{i\in\cube{4}}\kw'_i\,b(j;(i,p))$. Then
\begin{equation}\label{keccak:eq:at-s}
G(\kpt)=\sum_{p\in\cube{\kbatch-4}}\eq(r,p)\sum_{j<1600}\kpw_j\Bigl(\kwsum'\,S_{p,j}(\kpt)+\bigl(\kwsum'+S_{p,\along{j}{1}}(\kpt)\bigr)\,S_{p,\along{j}{2}}(\kpt)\Bigr).
\end{equation}

\paragraph{3. Packs.} Steps 3 and 4 are one sumcheck on \eqref{keccak:eq:at-s}, of $\kbatch+7$ rounds, with running claims $\rc{k}$ from $\rc{0}=G(\kpt)$ (\S\ref{sec:prelim}). Its first $\kbatch-4$ rounds bind $p$, with challenges $\fc^{\mathrm{pack}}$; the summand being $\eq(r,\cdot)$ times degree two, a round costs two $\E$-elements (\S\ref{sec:prelim}):
\begin{equation}\label{keccak:eq:after-packs}
\rc{\kbatch-4}=\eq(r,\fc^{\mathrm{pack}})\sum_{j<1600}\kpw_j\Bigl(\kwsum'\,S_{\fc^{\mathrm{pack}},j}(\kpt)+\bigl(\kwsum'+S_{\fc^{\mathrm{pack}},\along{j}{1}}(\kpt)\bigr)\,S_{\fc^{\mathrm{pack}},\along{j}{2}}(\kpt)\Bigr).
\end{equation}

\paragraph{4. Positions.} The other $11$ rounds bind $j\in\cube{11}$ (slice low, lane high; $\kpw_j$ and $S_{p,\along{j}{k}}$ are zero at lanes $25$ to $31$), with challenges $\fc^{\mathrm{pos}}$; the summand having degree three, a round costs three $\E$-elements. The prover sends $\kev_k=\sum_{j<1600}\eq(\fc^{\mathrm{pos}},j)\,S_{\fc^{\mathrm{pack}},\along{j}{k}}(\kpt)$ for $k=0,1,2$, and the verifier, computing $\mle{\kpw}(\fc^{\mathrm{pos}})$ itself, checks
\begin{equation}\label{keccak:eq:terminal}
\eq(r,\fc^{\mathrm{pack}})\;\mle{\kpw}(\fc^{\mathrm{pos}})\;\bigl(\kwsum'\,\kev_0+(\kwsum'+\kev_1)\,\kev_2\bigr)\ \qeq\ \rc{\kbatch+7}.
\end{equation}

\paragraph{5. Batching.} The verifier samples $\kbeta$ and sets $c'=\kev_0+\kbeta\,\kev_1+\kbeta^{2}\,\kev_2$, so that, moving the shifts onto the weights,
\begin{equation}\label{keccak:eq:flat}
c'=\sum_{j<1600}\kpw^{\flat}_j\,S_{\fc^{\mathrm{pack}},j}(\kpt),\qquad
\kpw^{\flat}_j=\eq(\fc^{\mathrm{pos}},j)+\kbeta\,\eq(\fc^{\mathrm{pos}},\along{j}{4})+\kbeta^{2}\,\eq(\fc^{\mathrm{pos}},\along{j}{3}),
\end{equation}
and, as $S_{\fc^{\mathrm{pack}},j}(\kpt)=\sum_{p\in\cube{\kbatch-4},\,i\in\cube{4}}\eq(\fc^{\mathrm{pack}},p)\,\kw'_i\,b(j;(i,p))$ (Fact~\ref{fact:eq}), $(c',\kw',\fc^{\mathrm{pack}},\kpw^{\flat})$ is a claim of the shape \eqref{keccak:eq:claim} on $b$.

\paragraph{6. Linear map.} As $\sum_{j<1600}\kpw^{\flat}_j\,b(j;t)=\sum_{j<1600}(\klin^{\top}\kpw^{\flat})_j\,a_{23-d}(j;t)$, the layer outputs $(c',\kw',\fc^{\mathrm{pack}},\klin^{\top}\kpw^{\flat})$ at depth $d+1$.

\subsection{The opening}\label{keccak:openings}

The claim $(c,\kw,r,\kpw)$ at depth $24$ is on the committed inputs:
\begin{equation}\label{keccak:eq:in}
c=\sum_{i\in\cube{4}}\kw_i\sum_{o\in\cube{5}}\sum_{z<64}\kpw^{\mathrm{in}}(o,z)\;\mle{Q_z}\bigl(i,r,o\bigr),\qquad
\kpw^{\mathrm{in}}(\ell,\,z)=\kpw_{z+64\ell},
\end{equation}
$\kpw^{\mathrm{in}}$ being zero on slots $25$ and above. A degree-two sumcheck binds $i$ (challenges $\fc^{\mathrm{low}}\in\E^{4}$), then the slot (challenges $\fc^{\mathrm{in}}\in\E^{5}$). The prover sends $s_0,\dots,s_{63}$ and the verifier checks the final claim against $\mle{\kw}(\fc^{\mathrm{low}})\sum_z\mle{\kpw^{\mathrm{in}}}(\fc^{\mathrm{in}},z)\,s_z$, leaving
\begin{equation}\label{keccak:eq:in-claim}
s_z=\mle{Q_z}\bigl(\fc^{\mathrm{low}},r,\fc^{\mathrm{in}}\bigr)\qquad(z<64),
\end{equation}
which ring switching takes (Annex~\ref{annex:rs}).

\subsection{Protocol summary}\label{keccak:summary}

\begin{protocol}[Keccak by GKR]\label{keccak:protocol}
\leavevmode
\begin{enumerate}
\item The verifier samples $r_{\mathrm{lane}}$ (\S\ref{keccak:claims}).
\item For $d=0,\dots,23$ (\S\ref{keccak:layer}): the prover sends $G$; the verifier checks \eqref{keccak:eq:projection} and samples $\kpt$; $\kbatch-4$ sumcheck rounds over packs and $11$ over positions; the prover sends $\kev_0,\kev_1,\kev_2$; the verifier checks \eqref{keccak:eq:terminal} and samples $\kbeta$.
\item The opening (\S\ref{keccak:openings}): $4+5$ sumcheck rounds, then $s_0,\dots,s_{63}$.
\end{enumerate}
Ring switching (Annex~\ref{annex:rs}) turns \eqref{keccak:eq:in-claim} into one claim for the commitment scheme (\S\ref{sec:e2e-unrolled}).
\end{protocol}

\subsection{Cost}\label{keccak:cost}

A layer sends $47+2(\kbatch-4)+33+3=2\kbatch+75$ elements of $\E$ and the opening $82$: $2650$ for $\kbatch=16$.

The prover's witness is bit-sliced, $64$ permutations to a machine word. It computes $G$ on bits: two carry-less products per pack and position, $\kemb_{\allone}(S_{p,j}+S_{p,\along{j}{2}})$ and $S_{p,\along{j}{1}}S_{p,\along{j}{2}}$. The first four rounds of step 3 are computed from bits too~\cite{Hashcaster}. After $\kpt$ it works in the basis $1,\kpt,\kpt^2,\dots$, in which $S_{p,j}(\kpt)$ has the coefficients of $S_{p,j}$ as coordinates, so its weighted sums are carry-less products; that basis exists unless $\kpt$ lies in a proper subfield of $\E$, where the prover aborts, with probability below $2^{-95}$ a layer.

\subsection{Weights in the recursion}\label{keccak:recursion}

The recursive verifier (\S\ref{sec:recursion}) never writes the $1600$ weights $\klin^{\top}\kpw^{\flat}$. By \eqref{keccak:eq:flat}, $\kpw^{\flat}$ is a lane table times a slice table:
\[
\kpw^{\flat}_{z+64\ell}=\kpw^{\mathrm{ln}}_{\ell}\,\kpw^{\mathrm{sl}}_{z},\qquad
\kpw^{\mathrm{sl}}_z=\eq(\fc^{\mathrm{pos}}_{<6},z),\qquad
\kpw^{\mathrm{ln}}_\ell=\eq(\fc^{\mathrm{pos}}_{\ge6},\ell)+\kbeta\,\eq(\fc^{\mathrm{pos}}_{\ge6},\along{\ell}{4})+\kbeta^{2}\,\eq(\fc^{\mathrm{pos}}_{\ge6},\along{\ell}{3}).
\]
(The first claim's weights factor too: lane table $\eq(r_{\mathrm{lane}},\ell)$, slice table $x^z$, and no $\klin^{\top}$.) Each use is a pairing $\inner{\klin^{\top}\kpw^{\flat}}{\kpair}=\inner{\kpw^{\flat}}{\klin(\kpair)}$ with $\kpair_{z+64\ell}=\kpair^{\mathrm{ln}}_\ell\kpair^{\mathrm{sl}}_z$: in \eqref{keccak:eq:terminal}, $\kpair$ is the next layer's $\eq(\fc^{\mathrm{pos}},\cdot)$; in \eqref{keccak:eq:projection}, it is the round constant, on lane $0$ and nonzero only at slices $2^k-1$; in the opening, $\kpair^{\mathrm{sl}}_z=s_z$ and $\kpair^{\mathrm{ln}}_\ell=\eq(\fc^{\mathrm{in}},\ell)$. With $\kto{\ell}$ the lane pi sends $\ell$ to, $w_\ell=\ell\bmod5$ and $\kpair^{\mathrm{ln}}_{[w]}=\sum_{k<5}\kpair^{\mathrm{ln}}_{w+5k}$ ($w$ taken modulo $5$),
\begin{equation}\label{keccak:eq:form}
\inner{\kpw^{\flat}}{\klin(\kpair)}=\sum_{\ell<25}\kpw^{\mathrm{ln}}_{\kto{\ell}}\Bigl(\bigl(\kpair^{\mathrm{ln}}_{\ell}+\kpair^{\mathrm{ln}}_{[w_\ell-1]}\bigr)\,\krot_{\koff{\ell}}+\kpair^{\mathrm{ln}}_{[w_\ell+1]}\,\krot_{\koff{\ell}+1}\Bigr),\qquad
\krot_n=\sum_{z<64}\kpair^{\mathrm{sl}}_z\,\kpw^{\mathrm{sl}}_{(z+n)\bmod64}.
\end{equation}
In \eqref{keccak:eq:terminal}, $\kpair^{\mathrm{sl}}$ is an $\eq$ table and $\krot_n$ is the multilinear extension of $[\,z'=z+n\bmod64\,]$ at the two slice points, evaluated by a carry ripple over six bits; for the round constants, the at most seven terms are added; in the opening, the $s_z$ are folded against the slice point one bit at a time, for every $n$ at once.

\subsection{Committing the middle state}\label{keccak:segments}

The VM also commits each permutation's state after twelve rounds, which halves the layers the verifier (and the recursion guest) runs.

\paragraph{Statement.} Segment $m<2$ is rounds $12m+1$ to $12m+12$. Slot $25m+\ell$ of $\qkeccak$ holds lane $\ell$ of segment $m$'s input: six slot variables, $\nkeccak=\kbatch+6$, slots $50$ to $63$ zero. With $a_{m,e}$ the state after $e$ rounds of segment $m$ from its committed input, $\kout{\ell}$ is read off $a_{1,12}$, and the statement is \eqref{keccak:eq:statement} and
\begin{equation}\label{keccak:eq:chain}
a_{0,12}(z+64\ell;\,t)=Q_z\bigl(t,\,25+\ell\bigr)\quad\text{for all }t,\ \ell<25,\ z<64.
\end{equation}

\paragraph{Layers.} A claim's point gains a last coordinate, the segment: $r\in\E^{\kbatch-3}$, \eqref{keccak:eq:claim} also sums over $m$ with $\eq(r,(p,m))$, on $a_{m,12-d}$, and layer $d<12$ goes through round $12(m+1)-d$ of each segment. The constant term of \eqref{keccak:eq:projection} becomes $\kwsum\sum_m\eq(r_{\kbatch-4},m)\sum_z\krc_{12(m+1)-d}[z]\,\kpw_z$, and the sumcheck gains a round after the pack's: $\fc^{\mathrm{pack}}\in\E^{\kbatch-3}$, its last coordinate the next claim's segment, and \eqref{keccak:eq:terminal} ends on $\rc{\kbatch+8}$. The claim at depth $12$ is on the committed inputs $a_{m,0}$.

\paragraph{The first claim.} Once the $\koutv{\ell}$ are in the transcript, the verifier samples $r_{\mathrm{seg}}$, then $r_{\mathrm{lane}}$; the first claim, at $(r_{\mathrm{pack}},r_{\mathrm{seg}})$, has the weights of \S\ref{keccak:claims} on both segments. Its part on segment $1$ is $r_{\mathrm{seg}}\sum_\ell\eq(r_{\mathrm{lane}},\ell)\,\koutv{\ell}$. With $\kmid{\ell}(t)=\sum_z a_{0,12}(z+64\ell;t)\,x^z$, the prover sends its part on segment $0$,
\[
c_{\mathrm{st}}=\eq(r_{\mathrm{seg}},0)\sum_{\ell<25}\eq(r_{\mathrm{lane}},\ell)\,\mle{\kmid{\ell}}(r_{\mathrm{low}},r_{\mathrm{pack}}),
\]
and $c=c_{\mathrm{st}}+r_{\mathrm{seg}}\sum_\ell\eq(r_{\mathrm{lane}},\ell)\,\koutv{\ell}$. A word determines its bits, so \eqref{keccak:eq:chain} says $\kmid{\ell}$ is slot $25+\ell$ of $\qkeccak$, which \eqref{keccak:eq:out} checks at the random point.

\paragraph{Openings.} First,
\begin{equation}\label{keccak:eq:out}
c_{\mathrm{st}}\ \qeq\sum_{o\in\cube{6}}\kpw^{\mathrm{out}}(o)\;\mle{\qkeccak}\bigl(r_{\mathrm{low}},r_{\mathrm{pack}},o\bigr),\qquad
\kpw^{\mathrm{out}}(25+\ell)=\eq(r_{\mathrm{seg}},0)\,\eq(r_{\mathrm{lane}},\ell),
\end{equation}
$\kpw^{\mathrm{out}}$ zero elsewhere, by a degree-two sumcheck over the slot (challenges $\fc^{\mathrm{out}}$), after which the prover sends $c_{\mathrm{out}}$ and the verifier checks the last running claim against $\mle{\kpw^{\mathrm{out}}}(\fc^{\mathrm{out}})\,c_{\mathrm{out}}$, leaving for the main protocol's pool
\begin{equation}\label{keccak:eq:out-claim}
\mle{\qkeccak}\bigl(r_{\mathrm{low}},r_{\mathrm{pack}},\fc^{\mathrm{out}}\bigr)=c_{\mathrm{out}}.
\end{equation}
Then the claim at depth $12$ is opened as in \S\ref{keccak:openings}, with $r_{<\kbatch-4}$ for $r$, $\kpw^{\mathrm{in}}(25m+\ell,z)=\eq(r_{\kbatch-4},m)\,\kpw_{z+64\ell}$, six slot rounds, and in \S\ref{keccak:recursion} $\kpair^{\mathrm{ln}}_\ell=\sum_m\eq(r_{\kbatch-4},m)\,\eq(\fc^{\mathrm{in}},25m+\ell)$.

\subsection{Proof of the embedding lemma}\label{keccak:rmfe-proof}

\begin{proof}
The \emph{readings} of $h$ of degree at most $D$ are $h(0)$, $h(1)$ and its coefficient of $X^D$; those of $hh'$, of degree at most $D+D'$, are the products of those of $h$ and $h'$. Take the seven pairwise coprime irreducibles $g$ below, of product $F$, of degree $23$:
{\setlength{\arraycolsep}{3pt}
\[
\begin{array}{c|ccccccc}
g & X & X+1 & X^3+X+1 & X^3+X^2+1 & X^5+X^2+1 & X^5+X^3+1 & X^5+X^3+X^2+X+1\\\hline
\deg g & 1 & 1 & 3 & 3 & 5 & 5 & 5\\
n_g & 1 & 1 & 2 & 2 & 3 & 3 & 3
\end{array}
\]}
Assign $u_0,\dots,u_{14}$ to the moduli in order, $n_g$ bits each, and let $h_g(u)$, linear in $u$, be the polynomial of degree below $n_g$ whose first $n_g$ readings, with $D=n_g-1$, are the bits assigned to $g$. As $2(n_g-1)<\deg g$, $h_g(u)\,h_g(v)$ is reduced modulo $g$. Put $\kemb(u)=H_u+u_{15}F$, with $H_u$ of degree below $23$, linear in $u$, and $H_u\equiv h_g(u)\pmod g$ for every $g$ (Chinese remainder theorem). Let $\kproj(P)$ be, for each $g$, the first $n_g$ readings of $P\bmod g$ with $D=2(n_g-1)$, then the coefficient of $X^{46}$. For $P=\kemb(u)\kemb(v)$, $P\equiv h_g(u)h_g(v)\pmod g$ gives the first fifteen products, and $P=H_uH_v+u_{15}FH_v+v_{15}FH_u+u_{15}v_{15}F^2$, whose first three terms have degree at most $45$ and $F^2$ is monic of degree $46$, gives $u_{15}v_{15}$ as the coefficient of $X^{46}$.
\end{proof}
