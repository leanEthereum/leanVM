# Soundness: the composition into one round-by-round statement

The library `Soundness` of the Lake project in the parent directory. It joins the pieces of the other libraries (`DeepFold`: Lemma 4.10 of the paper; `Protocol`: Lemma 4.11, Lemma 4.12, the parameter arithmetic; `Rounds`: the per-message transitions of the invariant and the extractor step; `Interleave`: the reduction of the list hypothesis to single-lane words) into one theorem: an abstract model of Protocol 4.2 with one batch of points per level, on a ladder of levels with its tail, is round-by-round sound with the errors of Theorem 5.2, and therefore sound and knowledge sound (in the relativised form of Theorem 5.6) with their sum. Section 8.2 of the paper states the composed theorem in words and says where the model is abstract; Section 8.4 says what is not covered. The project's `README.md` has the conventions, the notation and the build.

`Hypothesis.lean` instantiates the soundness theorem with every hypothesis discharged, on one concrete level with no tail and an explicit number (`protocolD_sound_concrete_unique_le`) and on two concrete levels with a tail (`protocolD_sound_concrete_ladder`); neither is a deployed parameter set. `Sound.lean` shows that prover strategies exist, for the first instance (`strategy_nonempty_one_level`) and on every ladder and with every tail that satisfy two conditions on the abstract maps (`strategy_nonempty_ladder`).

## Conventions

- A position of the protocol holds what the messages so far determine: the parameters of the current level and of the levels to come, the committed word folded by the challenges so far, the points, the carried values, the running claim, and two things only the analysis reads (the extraction predicate `G` and the proposition `tru`). The state function is a predicate on positions, so it does not change with a prover message, and property (iii) reads "from a doomed position, whatever the prover sends".
- A probability is `Protocol.dens`, a number of verifier messages over the number of messages, in `ℚ`. Query positions are the vectors `Fin t → D` with the domain `D` as a type.
- The error of a verifier message is a function `err` of the position it is sent from. It depends on the kind of the position and on the level's parameters only.
- The analysis constants are `τ`, `K₀` and `M`, with `tripleThr K₀ M = K₀ (K₀ - 1) (K₀ - M)` the threshold of the triple count of Definition 4.8. The hypotheses on them are `0 < τ`, `2 q ≤ M` and `0 < tripleThr K₀ M`.
- The names `Protocol.half₀` and `Rounds.half₀` (and `half₁`) are two definitions of the same thing; this library opens neither.
