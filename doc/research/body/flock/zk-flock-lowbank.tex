\subsection{A long-chain repair for the low query region}\label{note:zk-flock-lowbank}

\paragraph{Result.} A disjoint bank of $3840$ valid compression cycles jointly hides one sixteen-point low coset and one thirty-two-point high coset of lane $59$, together with the twelve final GKR children, all BLAKE2s terminal claims, established Flock endpoints, memory envelope and shared root, below $2^{-132}$. Independent count columns separate the two regions; this is one joint forty-eight-value theorem, not independent per-coset simulators. The high coset alone has error below $2^{-151}$. Public heights and verifier equations are unchanged. Arbitrary query families, common completion and the other full-view obligations remain open.

\subsubsection{An explicit low-coordinate source}

Start with the wider library of \noteref{zk-flock-children}. Among physical BLAKE2s positions below $2^{14}$, select every aligned sixteen-row block whose rows are outside that library, the mandatory binary source and the five protected general-input positions. There are exactly $240$ such blocks; their bases are below $12288$. Sort the $56$ bases whose bit four is zero and separately the $184$ whose bit four is one. Pair the former with the first $56$ of the latter, then pair the remaining lower $64$ with the remaining upper $64$. Enumerate these $120$ pairs in that order as $\zkLowPairs=\{(u_j,v_j):0\le j<120\}$. The only other free low positions are $437,438,439$, which remain unused. This public balanced matching replaces the earlier sorted-half matching discussed below; it uses exactly the same rows.

Use one fresh $32$-cell frame at exponent $1280+32\cdot65535$, compression code location $1090$ and its closing return at $1091$. The frame and code are disjoint from the earlier libraries. Repeatedly execute the same baseline compression/return cycle, with return controls at offsets $16,17,18$. For pair number $n=16j+t$, place its two compressions at $u_j+t,v_j+t$, for $t<16$. Each of the ten compression count columns then has labels $\gen^{2n},\gen^{2n+1}$. Independently swap these two labels in every column. The $3840$ reads of each operand and code location form one complete chain; the return rows and their labels remain fixed. All choices preserve every local constraint, bus multiset, count-column product, memory image and compression witness.

The bank replaces $3840$ general-input positions, leaving $57608$, and adds two code locations for a total of $64$. Sharing one frame instead of $3840$ distinct frames leaves at most $177413$ Flock padding frames, or $5677216$ cells. The remaining payload fits $26960$ slots of size $256$. These are reservations for the same public height profile, not a completed common-sampler theorem.

\subsubsection{Sixteen uniform raw values and a retained terminal claim}

For $x+\subsp_4\subseteq\subsp_{18}\setminus\subsp_{13}$, all lane-$59$ blocks except $\mathsf{cnt\_cv1}$ have zero encoder weight. A swap in the new bank's child slot $t$ contributes to its low coefficient coordinate the scalar $\gen^{2t}\zkLowWeights_j(x)$, where
\[
 \zkLowWeights_j(x)=(1+\gen)\gen^{32j}
       \bigl(\novel_{u_j}(x)+\novel_{v_j}(x)\bigr).
\]
The native exhaustive certificate proves binary rank $64$ for these $120$ scalars at all $32256$ sixteen-point cosets in $\subsp_{19}\setminus\subsp_{13}$, including all $15872$ cosets in the low region under consideration, and checks the same polynomial through the actual additive NTT. Independent bits for each $t$ therefore make all sixteen coefficient coordinates, and hence all sixteen raw values, exactly uniform. We must still account for the terminal $\mathsf{cnt\_cv1}$ claim made from those same bits.

Split the honest terminal row point into its four low physical coordinates and fourteen high coordinates. In the latter variables define
\[
 \zkLowHighPoly_j(z)=(1+\gen)\gen^{32j}
    \bigl(\eq(z,u_j/16)+\eq(z,v_j/16)\bigr).
\]
These $120$ degree-at-most-$14$ polynomials are linearly independent over $\K$, because their pairs of Boolean support points are disjoint. Fix $x$ and take a binary basis of the $56$-dimensional kernel of the rank-$64$ query map. Applying the same combinations to $\zkLowHighPoly_j$ gives $56$ independent polynomials. Their Moore determinant is nonzero and has degree at most $14(2^{56}-1)$: otherwise a nonzero linearized polynomial of degree at most $2^{55}$ over the rational-function field would vanish on a binary space of size $2^{56}$. Thus, outside probability $14(2^{56}-1)/2^{192}$, their evaluations span a binary space $\zkLowSpan\subseteq\E$ of dimension $56$.

Every child slot has this same kernel image, up to its nonzero factor $\gen^{2t}$. After excluding low terminal coordinates in $\{0,1\}$, normalize their equality weights. The four remaining independent ratios $\zkLowRatio{0},\ldots,\zkLowRatio{3}$ are uniform on $\E$ with two specified values excluded, after nonzero geometric rescaling. If $d=\dim(\zkLowSpan+\zkLowRatio{0}\zkLowSpan)$, then
\[
 \Pr[d\le a]\le
 \frac{(2^{56}-1)^2}{(2^{192}-2)(2^{112-a}-1)},
 \qquad 56\le a<112.
\]
Put $S=\zkLowSpan+\zkLowRatio{0}\zkLowSpan$. A character union bound using $S$ and its three independent random multiples gives
\[
 h(a)=\min\left\{1,
 \left(\frac{2^{192}}{2^{192}-2}\right)^3 2^{192-4a}\right\}.
\]
These four subspaces are contained in the full sixteen-term terminal image. Averaging over $d$ proves a sufficient joint failure bound
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \zkLowBankError={}&\frac{14(2^{56}-1)+8}{2^{192}}+h(112)\\
 &+\sum_{a=56}^{111}
 \frac{(2^{56}-1)^2h(a)}{(2^{192}-2)(2^{112-a}-1)}
 <2^{-132}.
 \end{aligned}
\]
The sum and $h(112)$ together are below $2^{-166}$. On good terminal coins, the terminal claim is uniform on every query fiber. Therefore the new bank jointly supplies sixteen independent base-field uniforms and one extension-field uniform, except with probability $\zkLowBankError$, uniformly over the selected coset and every fixed complementary contribution.

\subsubsection{Joint composition with the actual children}

\begin{theorem}[The repaired low-coset boundary]\label{thm:zk-lowbank-interface}
Add this bank to the wider source, with a common valid completion respecting its disjoint reservation. The joint view consisting of all thirty-seven BLAKE2s terminal claims, twelve actual final GKR children, established Flock endpoints and all sixteen lane-$59$ values in one fixed coset in $\subsp_{18}\setminus\subsp_{13}$ has a witness-free simulator. Including the complete memory envelope and shared bus root, its error is below $2^{-132}$. The coset may be selected using only honest query indices.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Keep the old good-coin ledger of Theorem~\ref{thm:zk-flock-twelve-interface}, using its fixed auxiliary coset at $\gen^{18}$ solely to certify the old span events. These events in particular give the ordinary child and joint child/terminal count spans, without retaining any auxiliary query value. Split the replacement into three independent source groups.

First use the old count banks with $\mathsf{cnt\_cv1}$ excluded. They change no raw value in the selected low coset and no terminal $\mathsf{cnt\_cv1}$ value. They jointly hide the other nine terminal counts and the two count-dependent child directions in each of the four child slots. The pull/count minor using $\mathsf{cnt\_cv0}$ and $\mathsf{cnt\_out0}$ is nonzero of degree at most $20$; its block indices are $(190,192)$ in the pull channel and $(48,50)$ in the count channel. Charge another $20/2^{192}$ for this minor, conservatively retaining the preceding ledger's old minor term. The count image is still the plane $p+\gen q=0$, leaving each child residual $P_t+\gen Q_t$ fixed. Condition on every other bit during this replacement.

Next use only the new bank's $\mathsf{cnt\_cv1}$ bits. By the preceding argument, they jointly hide the raw sixteen-value vector and terminal $\mathsf{cnt\_cv1}$ claim. Their effect on the children lies entirely in the count planes already replaced by independent uniforms; they change neither the four residuals nor the nine terminal program/frame coordinates. Finally use the old operand/frame/PC sources to hide those program/frame coordinates and residuals. Their changes to the already replaced count, child-plane and raw coordinates are absorbed by the corresponding uniforms. The established old span events prove this last replacement exactly as before.

Now apply the memory/root and compression-value/Flock hybrids in their established order. All old and new metadata choices preserve the required multisets and Flock witness. The combined error is at most the old twelve-value ledger, plus $\zkLowBankError$, the additional $20/2^{192}$ and the memory/root term. Exact rational arithmetic bounds this sum below $2^{-132}$. No conditioning of an unconditional marginal theorem on the disclosed children or query values is used. The bound is uniform in $x$, so independent honest-query selection adds no error.
\end{proof}

\subsubsection{Two adjacent sixteen-point cosets jointly}

Fix $x+\subsp_5\subseteq\gen^{18}+\subsp_{18}$ and put
\[
 \zkThirtyTwoLow{d}=\sum_j f_{32j+d}\novel_{32j}(x),\qquad d<32.
\]
The thirty-two raw values are an invertible evaluation map of these coefficients. The block weights are one for $\mathsf{cnt\_cv1}$ and $\mathsf{cnt\_out0}$ and zero for the other changing blocks. All old non-count metadata changes cancel on this region. Core swaps fill the pairs $(c+16+8b,c+20+8b)$; wider swaps fill $(c+4b,c+4b+8)$, for $c<4,b<2$. Their sixteen independent kernel coordinates leave the following sixteen quotients fixed:
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \zkThirtyTwoLower{c}{b}&=\zkThirtyTwoLow{c+4b}+\zkThirtyTwoLow{c+4b+8},\\
 \zkThirtyTwoUpper{c}{b}&=\zkThirtyTwoLow{16+c+8b}+\zkThirtyTwoLow{20+c+8b}.
 \end{aligned}
\]
The two halves of each core or wider bank have the same rank-$64$ scalar maps already certified in \noteref{zk-flock-sixteen}, with the core map rescaled by the nonzero factor $\novel_{16}(x)^{-1}$. Thus their joint raw image is precisely the $16$-dimensional kernel of this quotient map over $\K$.

For the balanced new bank define
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \zkBalancedLowWeight{j}&=(1+\gen)\gen^{32j}\novel_{u_j}(x),&&j<56,\\
 \zkBalancedHighWeight{j}&=(1+\gen)\gen^{32j}
       \bigl(\novel_{u_j-16}(x)+\novel_{v_j-16}(x)\bigr),&&56\le j<120.
 \end{aligned}
\]
The native exhaustive certificate proves, at every one of the $8192$ cosets in this region,
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \operatorname{span}_{\Ftwo}\{\zkBalancedLowWeight{j},\gen^{16}\zkBalancedLowWeight{j}:j<56\}&=\K,\\
 \operatorname{span}_{\Ftwo}\{\zkBalancedHighWeight{j},\gen^8\zkBalancedHighWeight{j}:56\le j<120\}&=\K.
 \end{aligned}
\]
These are fixed query maps, not assertions about random extension points.

\begin{theorem}[Thirty-two high-region values with the VM boundary]\label{thm:zk-highbank-interface}
Under the same common-completion assumptions, all thirty-two raw lane-$59$ values in one coset inside $\gen^{18}+\subsp_{18}$ join the established terminal, final-child and Flock interface below $2^{-155}$, or below $2^{-151}$ including memory and the shared root. No additional rows, error terms or verifier equations are needed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Condition on all new-bank bits. The core replacement in Theorem~\ref{thm:zk-flock-twelve-interface} hides nineteen terminal metadata fields, twelve final GKR children and two upper kernel coordinates per child. Removing the nonzero factor $\novel_{16}(x)$ from its fixed query map changes neither the $128$-bit disclosure budget nor the $320$-bit joint budget with the earlier extension evaluation. Retain the sixteen lower coefficients and eight upper pair sums during this replacement, not the changing partners of the upper kernel coordinates. The two disjoint halves of each wider bank then fill two lower kernel coordinates, instead of only their sum. Each scalar map is onto, and all its terminal/child translations are absorbed by the first replacement. We have obtained sixteen independent kernel uniforms and the replaced boundary, while retaining the sixteen quotients and every new bit.

Use only the new $\mathsf{cnt\_cv1}$ bits; condition on the other new columns. For $j\ge56$, both endpoints have bit four set, so these swaps change no lower coefficient. Slots $t=c,c+4$ jointly fill $\zkThirtyTwoUpper{c}{0}$ using the second scalar span above; slots $t=c+8,c+12$ independently fill $\zkThirtyTwoUpper{c}{1}$. All eight upper quotients therefore become uniform while the lower quotients remain fixed.

Now use $j<56$. The left endpoint has bit four zero and the right endpoint has it set. Slots $t=c,c+8$ jointly fill $\zkThirtyTwoLower{c}{0}$ by the first scalar span; slots $t=c+4,c+12$ independently fill $\zkThirtyTwoLower{c}{1}$. Their upper, kernel and terminal/child changes are absorbed by the preceding uniforms. The two stages use disjoint pairs, and within each stage every displayed quotient uses disjoint slot bits. Thus all thirty-two coefficient coordinates and the preceding boundary are jointly uniform. Evaluate, discard the auxiliary bits and complete the established memory/root and compression-value/Flock hybrids. Both quotient replacements are exact, so the old error ledger is unchanged.
\end{proof}

Together with Theorem~\ref{thm:zk-lowbank-interface}, this also hides one entire sixteen-point coset chosen anywhere in $\subsp_{19}\setminus\subsp_{13}$ below $2^{-132}$. Select the corresponding simulator using the public query indices and discard unneeded high-region values. This argument treats the low and high cases as alternatives; the following theorem supplies a simultaneous view. No thirteen-query occupancy exception is needed for a selected high-region coset after this repair.

\subsubsection{A low coset and a high coset simultaneously}

\begin{theorem}[A split-region forty-eight-value boundary]\label{thm:zk-split-region-interface}
Under the same common-completion assumptions, jointly include all sixteen lane-$59$ values in any one coset of $\subsp_4$ inside $\subsp_{18}\setminus\subsp_{13}$ and all thirty-two values in any one coset of $\subsp_5$ inside $\gen^{18}+\subsp_{18}$. Together with the established terminal, final-child, Flock and memory/root interface, this view has a witness-free simulator below $2^{-132}$. Both cosets may be selected using only honest query indices. The source, public heights and error ledger of Theorem~\ref{thm:zk-lowbank-interface} are unchanged.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The two count columns separate the regions: $\mathsf{cnt\_out0}$ has zero encoder weight throughout the low region and weight one throughout the high region; $\mathsf{cnt\_cv1}$ has weight one in both. Their swap bits are independent. Use three disjoint source groups, with every unused bit fixed during each replacement.

First use all old core and wider count banks except $\mathsf{cnt\_cv1}$, together with the new bank's $\mathsf{cnt\_out0}$ bits. Their joint projection fills $\E^{17}\times\K^{32}$: the other nine terminal counts, the eight count-plane child coordinates and the thirty-two high values. To see this, repeat the count-only part of the preceding high-region proof with $\mathsf{cnt\_out0}$ last in every core bank. Its unweighted memory-count evaluation and high query maps are identical to those for $\mathsf{cnt\_cv1}$, so the $128$- and $320$-bit fixed-disclosure budgets are unchanged. The minor using $\mathsf{cnt\_cv0}$ and $\mathsf{cnt\_out0}$ still spans each child plane, with the same additional degree-$20$ exception charged in the low theorem. Wider $\mathsf{cnt\_out0}$ bits supply the lower kernel coordinates, and new $\mathsf{cnt\_out0}$ bits supply the sixteen quotients by the two exact balanced certificates. All terminal and child translations in these latter replacements are absorbed by the core uniforms. This entire group fixes the low raw values, terminal $\mathsf{cnt\_cv1}$, the nine terminal program/frame coordinates and the four child residuals.

Next use only the new $\mathsf{cnt\_cv1}$ bits. The low-region Moore argument fills $\E\times\K^{16}$ for its terminal claim and low raw values, except with probability $\zkLowBankError$. It fixes the other terminal counts, program/frame coordinates and child residuals. Its high-query and child-plane translations are absorbed by the first group's uniforms. Finally the old operand/frame/PC sources fill the remaining $\E^{13}$ of program/frame coordinates and residuals; all their count and raw-query translations are absorbed. These three projections have binary dimensions $5312$, $1216$ and $2496$, respectively, totaling $9024=31\cdot192+48\cdot64$.

Apply the established memory/root and compression-value/Flock hybrids in the same order as the low theorem. Its old good-coin ledger can now use the actual high coset instead of the fixed auxiliary one: all relevant bounds are uniform in that coset, and the unweighted count spans are common to the memory-count columns. The union of that ledger's exceptions, the same extra minor exception and the low Moore exception therefore has exactly the previously bounded total. Shared extension coins require no independence of these bad events. The source groups themselves use disjoint bits, so no conditional entropy is counted twice. Honest-query selection and discarding unqueried values preserve the bound.
\end{proof}

This theorem joins two nonadjacent query regions, not independent per-coset simulators. It does not cover two arbitrary low cosets, two arbitrary high cosets, or queries outside the displayed union. Proposition~\ref{prop:zk-scattered-support} exhibits a dispersed forty-two-point dependency of this very source, one query per sixteen-point coset, within an event below $2^{-266}$ at rate one. The full query-family obligation remains open.

\paragraph{Why the original matching needed replacement.} The earlier rule paired the lower $120$ sorted bases with the upper $120$. Exactly $30$ pairs then touched a base whose bit four was zero. On a high-region thirty-two-point coset, every old source fixes the eight lower quotients displayed above. Each receives contributions from only two slot families of $30$ bits; the equally weighted two count columns give the same directions, not twice the rank. Their joint binary rank is therefore at most $8\cdot60$, instead of $8\cdot64$. The full raw rank is at most $2016$, and the native check attains this bound at $x=\gen^{18}$. Reordering the old pair labels cannot fix this dimension deficit. The balanced matching uses all $56$ lower bases in distinct cross-half pairs and removes that obstruction.

For the original matching, the twenty-eight points $12288,\ldots,12315$ also give raw rank $1760$ out of $1792$ and a functional separating the private-pointer difference. Its first twenty-seven points have full rank $1728$; the complete thirty-two-point block has rank $1760$. The audit preserves this historical regression and checks that the balanced source fills the displayed low thirty-two-point view. There are $7936$ cosets of $\subsp_5$ in $\subsp_{18}\setminus\subsp_{13}$. For independent honest queries, the probability that any one receives at least twenty-eight distinct queried points is at most
\[
 7936\binom{32}{28}\frac{q(q-1)\cdots(q-27)}{D^{28}},
 \qquad D=2^{22+\rexp}.
\]
This is below $2^{-399},2^{-458},2^{-505},2^{-548}$ at rates one through four. The union bound chooses the coset, its twenty-eight distinct points and distinct query positions hitting them. It bounds this concentration event, not all rank defects of either matching. No theorem yet forces an arbitrary distinguishing query set to have this concentration.

\paragraph{Evidence and boundary.} \auditref{zk_flock_lowbank_audit.py} enumerates the reservation and balanced matching, checks all $32256$ sixteen-point scalar maps through the native NTT, and checks both thirty-two-point quotient spans at all $8192$ high-region cosets. With \texttt{--full}, it also verifies complete read chains, historical and repaired query regressions and the old-source/new-quotient projections. The exact low-region Moore and combined ledgers remain below $2^{-132}$. The full-query audit's \texttt{--joint-boundary --lowbank} mode checks the three split-region projections, of ranks $5312$, $1216$ and $2496$, including their required zero coordinates and child-plane invariance. With query seed $601$, low points $12288,\ldots,12303$ and high points $262144,\ldots,262175$, the complete native query/terminal/child matrix has rank $9024$ out of $9024$. These sampled extension-field checks support the algebra; the preceding span proof, not the sample, supplies the uniform statistical bound. Arbitrary query families, other lanes and PCS stages, omitted GKR/table/Flock messages, authentication and one common valid sampler remain unproved.
