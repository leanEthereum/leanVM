\section{Two shifted products with linear disclosures}

Write $Q=|\K|$, $q=|\E|$. Let $L:\K^3\to\E$ be linear with image dimension at least two, let $d\in\E^\times$, and fix offsets $b_i$ and linear maps $A_i:\K^3\to\E^h$. Independently sample $v_i\in\K^3$, conditional on $X_i=b_i+L(v_i)\notin\{0,d\}$, and put $Y=\sum_i A_iv_i$.

\begin{lemma}[Paired product mixing]
The joint law of $(\prod_{i=1}^tX_i,\prod_{i=1}^t(X_i+d),Y)$ is within distance
\[
 \eta_{t,h}=\frac12\sqrt{\bigl((q-1)^2-1\bigr)q^h}
 \left(\frac{2\sqrt q}{Q^2-2}\right)^t
\]
of two independent uniform elements of $\E^\times$, independent of the \emph{actual marginal law} of $Y$. No rank or uniformity assumption on $Y$ is needed.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For multiplicative characters $(\theta_1,\theta_2)\ne(1,1)$ and any additive character $\psi$ of $\E$, the complete sum satisfies
\[
 \left|\sum_{x\notin\{0,d\}}\theta_1(x)\theta_2(x+d)\psi(x)\right|
 \le 2\sqrt q.
\]
For nontrivial $\psi$ and both multiplicative characters nontrivial, choose a generator $\chi$ of their generated subgroup, of order $n$, and write $\theta_1=\chi^a$, $\theta_2=\chi^b$. Then $\gcd(n,a,b)=1$, so $Z^n-X^a(X+d)^b$ is absolutely irreducible. Weil's mixed bound applies with two distinct roots and additive polynomial $X$, giving $2\sqrt q$~\cite{winger}. With one multiplicative character trivial, a Gauss sum minus one omitted term is bounded by $\sqrt q+1$. For trivial $\psi$, Jacobi sums give at most $\sqrt q$, including inverse characters~\cite{conrad}.

For one word and an additive character of its observation, average over fibers of $L$. A nontrivial phase on $\ker L$ gives zero; otherwise it descends to an additive phase on $b_i+\operatorname{im}L$ and extends to $\E$. Expanding this coset's indicator in annihilating additive characters bounds the unnormalized sum by $2\sqrt q$. Excluding its at most two forbidden points leaves at least $Q^2-2$ elements. Thus every mixed coefficient with a nontrivial multiplicative pair has magnitude at most $2\sqrt q/(Q^2-2)$ per word.

Independence raises this bound to the $t$th power. On $(\E^\times)^2\times\E^h$, coefficients with both multiplicative characters trivial agree exactly with the proposed target, since it retains the actual $Y$ marginal. There are $((q-1)^2-1)q^h$ remaining coefficients. Parseval and Cauchy-Schwarz give the result.
\end{proof}

\section{The actual MUL pair and its exceptional events}

In the preceding note's supported layout, $N=2^{18}$ and bus depth is $22$. Reserve $t=40$ MUL slots for random $v_i\in\K^3$ multiplied by $(1,0,0)$ in fresh frames. Both child-$2$ blocks contain state, bytecode, input-$a$ and input-$b$, but not output-$c$. Their products are
\[
 T_+=A_+\prod_i X_i,\qquad T_-=A_-\prod_i(X_i+d),
 \qquad d=e_2(1+\gen),
\]
where $X_i$ is the push input-$a$ fingerprint and both cofactors contain no sampled word. Each input is read once: its pull count is $1$, versus $\gen$ on push. Boundary changes occupy seven linear fields: four push children and three memory evaluations. Pull changes equal push changes; counters are unchanged. Thus $h=7$.

Exclude fingerprint coins with a coordinate in $\{0,1\}$ or $\operatorname{rank}_{\K}L<2$: \path{zk-bus-root.tex} bounds this by $8/q+Q^2/(q-2)^2$, also excluding $d=0$. If both cofactors together contain at most $m$ other factors, their zeros cost $m/q$; sampled zeros cost $2t/q$, averaged over uniform $\beta$. Conditioning on no sampled zero preserves independence. The lemma then replaces the pair while retaining the actual boundary and complementary padding, at cost
\[
 \varepsilon_{\rm pair}(m)
 =\frac{m+2t+8}{q}+\frac{Q^2}{(q-2)^2}+\eta_{t,7}.
\]
This charges bad branches once: the target and real distributions have the same conditional boundary marginals on each branch. The nonzero shift is essential; at $d=0$ the two unscaled products coincide.

\paragraph{Joint simulator.} First replace the shared root using the original thirty-two unused words, retaining all forty MUL words and hence both selected children. Next replace the pair using the lemma, retaining the actual boundary. Finally apply \path{zk-bus-boundary.tex}, conditional on arbitrary fixed MUL data. The independent replaced root is carried through both later hybrids. If $\varepsilon_*$ denotes the original root-decoupling bound, the total error is
\[
 \varepsilon_{17}+\varepsilon_*(2^{22})
 +\varepsilon_{\rm pair}(8N-80)<2^{-155}.
\]
Here $\eta_{40,7}<2^{-376}$. The simulator samples honest coins, three independent uniform nonzero field elements for the root and pair, and seventeen independent uniform boundary fields. A compatible publicly normalized count root may be adjoined. Sampling independent roots for the \emph{entire} first packet would violate its product identities; the other children are outside this view.

\paragraph{Evidence and next obligation.} \path{zk_gkr_paired_audit.py} checks exact small-field distributions, including kernel-sensitive views and failure at zero shift; actual layouts, factors and linear disclosures; valid local cycles and reservations; and rational error bounds. No full-size trace is instantiated. Next include the other first-packet children with their shared factors and product identities, then the subsequent layers.
