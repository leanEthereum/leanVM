# Modedness of the guest's run

Question: the invariant `Inv` that `execute_complete` (`lean_vm/LeanVM/Prover/ExecuteComplete.lean`) needs on the deployed guest, so that every step is moded (`OpModed`: XOR's, MUL's, JUMP's, KECCAK's inputs and a DEREF's pointer written; a back-solved MUL's divisor nonzero) and every hint's inputs are written, the hints' values agreeing with `M` being the separate condition (a) of `exec-spec.md`.

Answer: a per-function forward walk of the compiled code (the executor's own `Op`s and `Hint`s, offsets from `fp`), over facts of the form "this cell is written", generated by `guest-cert` and kernel-checked per pack like `Cplw`. Write-once memory makes every fact permanent, so the domain has no kill sets and no aliasing problem. Heap cells are facts keyed by the frame cell holding the pointer, carried across calls by per-function signatures and across loop iterations by two fixed rules. The walk owns time; what it cannot decide is a residual value statement about the reference valuation (an index below a bound, a divisor nonzero), discharged from B1, which knows values but not times. The frames compose into `Inv` as a stack read from the frame headers. The walk and most of its soundness build (below).

## What has to hold, split

`OpModed M s op` and `HintOk C M s h` mix two kinds of facts. Mode facts: a cell is written in `s.mem` (`Mem.written`). Value facts: `M` at a cell is a power below `maxCells` (a DEREF's pointer, a taken JUMP's frame), a divisor is nonzero, a hint's output is what `M` holds. The first kind depends on the order of the run and is the subject here. Of the second kind, the DEREF pointer and JUMP frame facts follow from the reference step itself (`run` executes the instruction at `M`, so the pointer names a cell below `2^κ`), once for every program; the hint values are condition (a); a nonzero divisor is either a mode-domain fact (below) or a residual.

## The walk

State at an instruction (`Abs`): `w`, frame offsets written; `h`, pairs `(a, o)` meaning frame cell `a` is written, holds `g^b`, and cell `b + o` is written (a heap cell, or a callee frame's cell when `a` holds the callee's frame pointer); `k`, offsets holding a known constant (as `ExecCore`'s environment, which the walk reuses for jump targets and selector arms); `nz`, offsets known nonzero; range facts (below). `Holds s A` reads it relative to `s.fp` (`Mode/Frame.lean`).

Rules, per `Op` (the executor's): XOR and forward MUL require both inputs in `w` and add the output; SET adds its cell; a MUL whose output and one input are in `w` back-solves the other, requiring the known input in `nz`; KECCAK requires its thirteen inputs and adds its outputs; DEREF requires its pointer; in mode `cell`, if the frame side is in `w` or `(a, o)` is in `h`, both sides are written after the step, so it adds both (a store, a load, or a write-once assertion, the direction falling out of the state, never declared); otherwise it links and the walk learns nothing; modes `pc` and `fp` add `(a, o)`. JUMP requires its three cells. Hints: an allocation adds its pointer cell; a stream or computed hint requires its input cells (value, selector, size, loop bound, pointer, a `log2Ceil`'s bit cells or `(ptr, o)` facts) and adds its frame outputs, or `(ptr, lo + j)` for heap outputs. `nz` grows from SETs of nonzero constants, pointer cells after a DEREF through them (the executor's `logOr` succeeded, so the word is `g^b`), outputs of the `g^n`-valued hints and allocations, products and copies of `nz` cells, and the `assert !=` gadget.

Control: intra-function control flow is forward only (branches and `match` arms to a join; loops are tail-recursive helpers), so one pass suffices, with the intersection at joins, exactly as `ExecCore.exec` already segments and joins. A call (the shape `exec` recognizes: header and arguments stored through the callee frame pointer cell `q`, JUMP to a known entry) requires the callee's entry set as facts `(q, o)` and the callee's heap precondition translated through the arguments; after it, the walk adds `(q, o)` for the callee's return cells and the callee's postcondition translated. A fused dispatch requires the entry of every arm and keeps the intersection of their postconditions. The return JUMP requires the return cells in `w` and the function's postcondition in `h`.

## The heap

Pointer cells are normalized to monomials: a cell written by `MUL` of cells is the product of their monomials, a constant `g^k` factor a shift, so `buf·i^S·g^k` is `({buf: 1, i: S}, k)` and `h` is keyed by monomial and offset. Cells are write-once, so a frame offset names one value per instance; the monomials are as good as SSA names.

Signatures. Per function: `entry` (offsets its walk needs written at entry: header, used arguments, a loop's self frame cell), `pre` (heap facts over its parameters' monomials it needs at entry), `post` (heap facts over parameters and returned pointers it guarantees at return). Inferred bottom-up over the call graph by `guest-cert`; a loop helper is checked against its own signature at its tail call.

Loops. A loop helper with counter parameter `i`, stepping `i ↦ i·g^t`, and bound parameter `n`: at its tail call, a precondition fact `(m·i^S, o)` is either covered by the iteration's own writes at `(m·i^S, o + S·t)` (a chained fact, a value carried through the heap from one iteration to the next; 1442 sites) or else replaced by the range fact `Rng(m, S, o, [i, n))` (the cells `e(m) + S·j + o` for `e(i) ≤ j < e(n)`; 1805 sites); the next iteration's range is contained in this one's. Writes at `(m·i^S, o)` on the path to the tail call give `Rng(m, S, o, [i, n))` as a postcondition, the exit path giving the last point. Two generic lemmas, proved once.

Residuals. Where coverage needs a value fact, the walk records a side condition instead of failing: a range fact against a point or another range with different bound cells (`e(lo') ≤ e(lo)`, `e(hi) ≤ e(hi')`), an index from a hint or a load inside a filled table (`e(x) < e(n)`, unless a range-check gadget on `x` already gives it, which the walk uses), a pointer loaded from a table of pointers (it equals some buffer's base), a divisor not in `nz`. Each is a closed statement about the instance's values, listed per function in its signature (`sc`), true of every instance in the honest tree, and proved from B1, whose templates know the values (most are consequences of the function's own atoms and its B1 precondition, the soundness direction of its template). No side condition mentions time.

## Composition into `Inv`

`Inv s`: there is a stack of frames `(f₀ = main, 0), …, (fₙ, fp)` with walk positions, such that the top frame is `(f, s.fp)` at `s.pc` with its walk state holding at `s`, each suspended frame is at a call whose walk state (before the call) holds and whose callee frame pointer cell names the next frame, consecutive frames are linked by the callee's header (`M (fp' + 0) = g^ret`, `M (fp' + 1) = g^fp`, written by the caller's DEREFs of mode `pc` and `fp`, so the executor's cells agree with `M`), and each frame's side conditions hold of `M` at its frame. Every fact is monotone in `s.mem`, so a suspended frame's state needs no maintenance.

`InvRule` by cases on the top instruction: a non-jump is the walk's step (`walk_step`); a forward branch or table jump moves to the segment the reference takes, whose join state is contained in the edge's; a call pushes, the callee's entry facts being the caller's `(q, o)` facts read at the new `fp`, its side conditions from the hypothesis on the honest tree; a return pops, the header giving the caller's frame and return point, and the callee's return cells and postcondition becoming the caller's `(q, o)` facts; a loop's tail call replaces the top frame (the header is copied, so it returns to the loop's caller), its entry and precondition from the transport rule; `main`'s halting jump ends the run. Which branch, arm or callee runs is the reference's (`OnRef` and `Fine` give the executor the reference's registers), so `Inv` never decides a condition. The side-condition hypothesis needs every frame the run enters to be an instance of the honest tree with its valuation at its certificate cells: `FrameHolds` and `honest_kid` already say so for B2C's completeness, and that bridge is the one place the tree enters.

## Measurements

A rough Python version of the heap part on the core IR (`Fn` bodies of `Guest/Pack/*/Core*.lean`; a measurement, not a proof; scripts in the session scratchpad, not committed). Frame values: no use before definition outside loads, as `Fn.renumber`'s order suggests, so the frame walk should accept the guest once the plumbing (header, callee frame pointers, jump targets, pooled constants, shared cells) is followed, which the compiled-level walk does by construction. Heap: 52,603 load-direction DEREFs and 14,091 stores; the domain above (monomials, signatures, chained and range loop rules) justifies 47,330 loads (90%) without side conditions. Unjustified: 5,273 load sites and 8,677 call-site requirements, in 415 of 1601 functions, concentrated in `verify_tables`, `verify_sub`, `verify_keccak`, the claim lists, `rs_eq_run` and DA. By the origin of the pointer's monomial, over both kinds of site: an index computed from a hint or a load (table lookups, about 5,600: side conditions, unless a range check on the index already bounds it); a pointer returned by a callee (about 3,900: a postcondition on returned pointers, which the crude version does not infer and the generator should, removes most); an own allocation read at constant or parameter offsets after a loop filled it (about 2,600: range-against-point coverage with constant or equal bounds, mostly syntactic); a pointer loaded from memory (about 1,100: side conditions); other shapes (about 750). MUL back-solves with a non-constant divisor: 696, of which 538 divide by products (mostly of `g` powers, `nz` by the product rule) and the rest by loaded values, Keccak outputs and parameters (challenges: residual side conditions, or a completeness exception the headline must already account for).

## Alternatives

At the core IR instead of the ISA: the IR is close to SSA, but modedness is about the executor's instructions, plumbing included (header, callee frame pointers, return and jump targets, pooled constants, shared cells, range-check temporaries), and a core-level fact would need a B2C-style simulation to reach `runOp`. At the ISA, a DEREF's direction comes from the walk's own state, and the theorem is about the executor with no bridge for frames.

Extending B2C's completeness walk (`B2C/Cpl*`): it files a block's atoms before walking and reasons about a final memory image, so adding order means redoing its hardest proofs (`CplWalk.lean`) over partial memories. The new walk reuses `ExecCore.exec`'s segments, shapes and joins, not `Cpl`'s proofs.

A tree-recursive executor lemma per instance (as `CplRun`) instead of a step invariant: natural for calls, but a second completeness theorem beside `execute_complete`; the header-chain stack keeps `InvRule` and needs the tree only for side conditions.

Heap by B1 alone: give `CompleteAt`'s layouts a direction (read versus filled) and prove "filled by return". B1 does not distinguish loads from stores (a store is the same `load` atom), so every B1 proof with a buffer changes, and the order inside a function still needs a walk. The split puts order in generated code and leaves B1 only value facts.

Heap by a full symbolic analysis (separation logic with symbolic intervals): data-dependent indices (table lookups, pointer tables) defeat it; the side conditions are where it would stop anyway.

An order-free executor (propagate to a fixpoint, or read an unwritten cell as deferred): breaks the byte-for-byte mirror of the Rust executor, and its completeness becomes the acyclicity of the run's dependencies, no easier. Per-pc annotations (proof-carrying code): unneeded, intra-function flow being acyclic; recomputation costs what checking would.

## The pieces

1. Structured hints: `guest-export` writes each function's hint lists as data in its pack (as `Bundle` holds its code), with one kernel check that they are `Data.Advice`'s, or the Statement's `guestHints` is the term itself. Generator plus one link module; low risk; one cold heavy module.
2. `Mode/Abs.lean`: the domain and the walk over `Op` and `Hint` (tries keyed by `ℕ`, as `ExecCore`; monomials; signatures; call, dispatch, join, loop rules; side conditions as data). About 1,000 lines of definitions; medium risk, the expressiveness of the heap facts.
3. `Mode/Sound*.lean`: each rule sound against `runOp` and `runHint`. About 2,000 lines; low risk, mechanical like `ExecuteComplete*`.
4. `Mode/Inv.lean`: the stack invariant and `InvRule` from every function's acceptance and the side-condition hypothesis: calls, returns, tail calls, dispatch, joins, halting. About 1,500 lines; medium to high risk (the plumbing shapes; reuse `Exec`'s `Kind` lemmas).
5. `guest-cert`: signature inference (bottom-up, loops checked at their tail call), `Sig` extended with `entry`, `pre`, `post`, `sc`, per-pack `Modew*.lean` (`modeOkD … = true := by decide +kernel`, split in windows from snapshots as `Cplw` parts are) and `ModeOk` link modules; firewalled like `CertFirewall`. About 800 lines of generator; medium risk; build cost below.
6. Side conditions: per function with residuals, a lemma from its B1 module that every instance's valuation satisfies `sc`, plus the bridge that the run's frames are tree instances (`FrameHolds`). The count is the dominant unknown: after the generator infers returned-pointer postconditions and range coverage, expect a few hundred conditions in a few dozen families (table index bounds, pointer tables, challenge divisors); high risk, hand work, and the only part a guest change breaks by hand (`guest-changes` lists the functions).
7. `regen-guest.sh`: one step after `certificates` (the signatures and walks), with `--check`.

Order: 1 and 3 first (sound walk for frame cells and constant-offset heap facts, every function, no side conditions where none needed), then 5 to measure the real residual on the compiled code, then 4, then 6.

## The walk (M5a)

`lean_vm/LeanVM/Guest/Mode/Abs.lean` is the walk: the state (`w`, `v` words as products over bases `fr o` and `hp q d`, `nz`, heap words `hv`, heap cells `h`, runs `r`, exponent inequalities `lt`, the last products `pr`, sums `xs` by their cell), each instruction (`stepOp`: XOR, MUL forward and back-solved, SET, DEREF store/load/link, JUMP, KECCAK, the range-check and `!=` gadgets), each hint (`stepHint`, `allocAdvice` learning nothing: the allocation that follows writes its pointer), residuals (`Res.lt`, `Res.nz`, added at an offset), joins (`Abs.inter`), calls, dispatches, tail calls and returns through `Sig` (`entryOk`, `atCall`, `afterCall`, `tailOk`, `termStep`, `retOk`, `loopOk`, translated by `trBase`/`trMono`, exponents by `idxUp` out to a caller and `idxDown` in to a callee), and the pass (`walk`: at most two paths apart at each offset, more met into one by `cap`; the fall-through of a `JUMP` on a sum `[c] = [a] + [b]` learns `[a] = [b]`, `Abs.learn0`). Its meaning is `Mode/Sem.lean` (`Holds`, every fact existential in the words it reads, so monotone), its soundness `Mode/Sound{Step,Op,Deref,Hint,Join,Call,Entry,Loop}.lean`: every instruction (`stepOp_sound`), every hint (`stepHint_sound`, `stepHints_sound`), joins from either side and capping (`Holds.inter`, `Holds.inter_right`, `Holds.cap`), the fall-through (`Holds.learn0`), residuals, coverage by points and runs (`covered_sound`, `coveredR_sound`, `covered_val`); a callee's bases, products, exponents and runs translated (`trBase_iff`, `trMono_iff`, `trIdx_up`, `trIdx_down`: a shifted base's exponent moves out when the caller knows it and in when it is below `2^62`); a call's entry (`entryOk_sound`, from the callee's termination facts and the jump table's run, taken as hypotheses), its termination facts at the call (`atCall_sound`), its return (`afterCall_sound`, from what the callee guarantees, `RetHolds`, and its accumulated runs), a return (`retOk_sound`), a tail call (`tailOk_sound`, `termStep_sound`: the next counter is this one times `g^t`, `t > 0`, the bound the same word); termination giving every iteration's entry inequalities (`term_holds`), and a loop's accumulated run from the next iteration's and this iteration's point (`loop_step`, `RW.last`). Not yet proved: `Inv`, which discharges those hypotheses (the callee's termination facts from the loop's exit, a loop's last iteration from its exit condition) and walks the pass.

Runs of pointers (`Abs.p`; `Sig.preP`, `postP`, a loop's `postLP`): each cell of a run holds a word naming a cell whose given cells past it are written. A load from such a run writes those cells' facts for the loaded word (`ptrLoad`); a run is covered by a known run, a single point (`[i, i + 1)`, a loop's own record, chained from one iteration to the next) or its points enumerated (`coveredP`). Proved: `ptrLoad_sound`, `coveredP_sound`, `PW.trUp`, `PW.trDown`, `loop_stepP`, `PW.last`, and the entry, call, return and tail rules extended (`Mode/Sound{Deref,Call,Entry,Loop}.lean`).

`lean_vm/scripts/mode-cert/` mirrors the walk in Python (`walk.py`), infers signatures callee first (`infer.py`, about 12 s: pending edges share one snapshot and the meets of a join are memoized, which the many 256-arm dispatches of `__loop2` needed), and writes the certificates (`gen.py`, the `mode` step of `regen-guest.sh`): each pack's signatures (`Pack/*/ModeSig.lean`) and checks (`Pack/*/Mode{k}.lean`: each function's hints, residuals and table sizes as data, and `walk … = true` by `decide +kernel`), firewalled by `Guest/Mode/Firewall.lean`. A function counts as closed when it passes with its inferred signature and so do its callees, transitively (a failing callee gets an empty signature, which lets its callers pass vacuously). 1546 of 1601 functions are closed, 23 of them with residuals (46 exponent bounds, 7 nonzero divisors). Of the 41 functions failing on their own: 16 `child_claim_list_mono_last` specializations and 3 loops read a table at an index from a hint or a load with no bound the walk can state (a residual needs the table's length as an exponent), 11 `open_stacked` specializations pass a callee a cell behind a pointer the walk does not track, and 11 other. The kernel's walk is strict (`Abs.force`, `foldlA`), pending edges go in groups by their targets (`Grp`, a group of three or more paths met at once, so a 256-arm dispatch's arms share one state), a join keeps the states' common tail whole (`interT`), a heap run a hint writes is one fact, a callee's returned words over its parameters cross the call (`Sig.retV`, which moved the `open_stacked` failures past their second `opening_round` call), and a signature's 8 or more consecutive points are a run (a Merkle cap's whole precondition). A long or costly function is checked in windows (`Mode/Win.lean`, `walk_of_wins`): at a cut (`Fun.cuts`) the walk goes on from a generated snapshot weaker than its state (`cutAt`, `Abs.sub`, sound by `Holds.sub`), each window its own module (`Pack/*/ModeD*W*`), chained in `ModeL*`; `gen.py` cuts where the fewest states are pending and sizes windows and modules by an estimate of the kernel's list work (`walk.COST`). Each function's hints are proved the advice's (`Pack/*/ModeH`, `HintsOk`, `hintsOk_of_fn`) against `Data/AdviceHints.lean` (the advice as data, by function, written by `guest-export`; its parse of `Data.adviceText` is checked in `tests/LeanVMTests/Guest/AdviceData.lean`), and the executor's `Ctx.of` table at a pc is the advice's (`Mode/HintsCtx.lean`, `ctx_hintsOk`). 1546 functions are kernel-checked (all passing but `exponent_tables`, `__loop15` and `da_column_offsets`, whose list work is too dense, and `__loop17`, whose windows exceed 3.5 GB however short), 1544 with their hints (not `__loop2`, `sp_wots`: one `hintsChk` over their offsets is too costly). Cold cost of the 491 modules: about 64 CPU-minutes, `__loop2`'s windows 23 of them, each under 3.5 GB. The failures are as above but for `open_stacked`, now at its first table-dispatched call, whose arms' returned buffer the walk does not know filled. Not yet: `Inv`, and the residuals' proofs from B1.
