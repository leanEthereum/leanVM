# Modedness at the core IR

The guest's executability (on the honest hints every instruction finds its inputs written, `GuestExecutionComplete`) is proved from per-function facts about the zkDSL core IR and the compiled code, checked by the kernel, and generic theorems about the compilation proved once. It replaced the bytecode-level mode walk (`exec-mode.md`, now history): its kernel-checked walk over every compiled instruction with its snapshots (`Pack/*/Mode*`, `Mode/Certs*`), the residual certificates (`Res/`) and their proofs over bytecode offsets (`EndExecR*`), `Bridge/AlignWin` and the generators `scripts/mode-cert`, `scripts/res-cert` are deleted. Every compiled function is on the core route. Hand proofs cite core names (`.n k`, a function's stable name `Fn.«k»`), never bytecode offsets; what cites offsets is generated.

## Decomposition

Modedness is split by what each half needs to see.

* **Frame cells** (values, and the plumbing: header, callee frame pointers, jump targets, pooled constants, shared cells, range-check temporaries, hint cells) stay at the ISA: `CoreMode/Frame.lean`'s `frameOk`, one forward pass over the code as `Exec.exec` cuts it, a set of written frame offsets updated by the executor's own rules (`XOR`, `MUL` forward or back-solved by a divisor it knows nonzero, `KECCAK`, a `JUMP`'s cells, a `DEREF`'s pointer; a `DEREF` of mode `cell` stores when its frame side is written, loads when its heap side is known written or its offset is in the oracle `O`). It has no certificate of its own: its data is the function's interface (`FFun`), the oracle and stores from the pairing, and its residual divisors from its own walk.
* **Heap cells**, the only part that needs reasoning, are proved at the core: `CoreMode/HeapC.lean`'s `heapOkC`, one pass over the body over cells named by monomials of core values (`CCell`), each read's witnesses (`Wit`: an earlier event of its path, a precondition, a run, a residual) in a generated certificate (`HCert`). A call meets its callee's heap signature (`HSig`: points and runs over slots) and gains its guarantees; a loop helper's tail call meets its own preconditions, chained or covered.
* **The pairing** ties the two: `CoreMode/PairDefs.lean`'s `pairOk`, `Bridge.alignOk`'s in-order pass over code and body with the heap events consumed in order, each `DEREF` of a `load` at its certificate cells; it checks the oracle `O` (the offsets whose `DEREF` reads a cell the heap certificate proves written) and the stores `S` that the frame walk reads.
* **Residuals**: the statements on core names a heap certificate's witnesses use (`CRes`: `lt`, `sub`, `nz`), each of a family (`resFam`, `CoreMode/CoreRes.lean`), a decidable check on the function's own code whose soundness at its honest instances is proved once per family from B1.
* **Signatures** are contracts: each function's `Fn.«k».csig : Mode.Sig` (`CoreMode/Sig/<pack>.lean`) is kept as written, its frame cells checked against the frame layout and held by the route's checks to its heap signature (`CoreMode.ownOk`, `fitB`); a new function's, or one whose frame layout changed, is given by hand in `scripts/core-mode/sigseed.py`.

## The generic theorems

* `Mode/Spec.lean`, `FrameSpec`: what the stack invariant needs of one function, for any frame predicate `Q`; `Mode/InvRule.lean`'s `inv_rule_of_specs` builds `InvRule` from every function's, and `execute_complete_guest` follows.
* `CoreMode/CoreSound.lean`, `frameSpec_core`: a function whose core data its checks accept (`CoreOk`: `heapOkC`, `pairOk`, `frameOk` with its windows' `FrameAcc`, the signature's `ownOk`, `retVOk`, `shapeOk`, `jumpCallOk`, `jtdOk`) meets `FrameSpec` at the core route's frame predicate `CoreAt` (the frame's honest valuation, its cells written as the passes say), wherever its frames have honest valuations.
* `CoreMode/HeapCSound.lean`, `heapOkC_sound`: every read of a path is written by the events before it, the guarantees written at return; `HeapCTree.lean` lifts it over an honest call tree. `PairSound.lean`, `FrameSound.lean`: the pairing's and the frame walk's passes give their facts at each offset they reach.
* Long functions are checked in windows from named states: `HeapCWin*` and `LoopWin` (the heap pass), `RouteWin` (the route's passes over the body), `PairRun` and `PairSlice` (the pairing: `pairOk_of_chainS`), `FrameWin` and `FrameSlice` (the frame walk: `chainFS_wins`). A window over a slice reads the code chunks it covers (`Bridge.slice`), not the code before it.
* `CoreMode/Assemble.lean`, `CoreFacts.of`, `assemble`: the generated certificates (`EndExec.CertE`, written one line each by `core_cert%`, `CoreMode/CertLit.lean`) closed into the end theorem's `ModeCerts`, with their termination facts and residuals; `OffFacts.nil`: no function off the route.
* `EndExecCore.lean`, `coreHonAll_X`: on the tracks' invariant each frame of a function is an honest instance, its valuation the core route's; `EndExecCoreRes.lean`, `resFam_inst`: each family's residuals hold there. `EndExecFinal.lean` wires them into `ExecGaps`, with no open fact (`openGaps`).

## The families

`resFam` (`CoreMode/CoreRes.lean`) lists them: `rc` (a value range checked against a constant on every returning path), `sel` (a top-level selector's arms), `exp` (an exponent an entry run bounds), `cs` (`column_selector`'s strided table), `addr` (addresses dereferenced and bits' powers), `rcv` and `rcn` (range checked against another value), `under` (beneath branch conditions), `split` (`g`-powers split from hinted bits, `EndExecCoreSplit.lean`), `neg`; the ghost families `ml` (`child_claim_list_mono_last`'s reads in its caller's table, `EndExecCoreMono.lean`) and `st` (a loop's reads past its cursor in its caller's proof stream, `EndExecCoreSt.lean`); and per-function facts: `verify_tables` (`EndExecCoreVt.lean`), `verify_keccak` (`EndExecCoreVk.lean`), `da_pad` (`EndExecCoreDa.lean`), the claim lists' linked callers (`EndExecCoreLink.lean`). Their mathematics on the reference is in `CoreMode/Fam/` (the definitions `*Defs`, the facts carried down the honest tree from the callers `CsQ`, `ExpQv`, `TermQ`, and the proofs `Res*`, which the old route stated over offsets and now state over core names).

## The census

Every compiled function is on the core route, taken by one of four checks (`route.py`'s `kind`, reported by `core.py`): a body with no call and no loop (`heap.py`), a body with calls (`callc.py`), a leaf loop helper (`loopc.py`), a loop helper with calls (`loopcc.py`). At the guest this note was written for: 275, 739, 320 and 214 of 1548 functions (`python3 -I scripts/core-mode/route.py` prints the counts at the current guest, with a fresh `CORE_MODE_CACHE`); a function no check takes is reported there and fails `core.py`.

## Per-function data and what a guest edit regenerates

Each function's data, by pack, is written by `regen-guest.sh`'s `core-mode` step (`scripts/core-mode/`): its signature and entry (`sigc.py`: `CoreMode/Sig/`, `GlueE/`), its powers of `g` and glue to its global index (`pows.py`, `glue.py`), its heap certificate and its callees' heap signatures (`callc.py`, `loopc.py`, `loopcc.py`, `heap.py`: `Gen/<pack>/Data*`, long ones in windows `GenW/`, `GenWL/`), its pairing's oracle and stores (`pair.py`: `Gen/<pack>/Pair`, a long function's windows `Gen/<pack>/<fn>/W*`), its frame walk (`frame_gen.py`: `Pack/<pack>/Frame*`, one `frame_fn%` a function, `CoreMode/FrameLit.lean`), its core data and route checks (`core.py`: `Gen/<pack>/Core`, `Route`), and its certificate with its facts (`certs.py`: `Gen/<pack>/Cert`, `Gen/Certs*`). The `bridge` step (`scripts/bridge-cert/`) writes the hint bridge's checks per function (`Bridge/Align`, `AllocChk` with `alloc_chk%`, `PairChk`, `WitChk`, `JumpCells`, `ProgAl`); the `res` step the callers' facts carried down (`monocall.py`, `carry.py`, `termcall.py`).

A one-function guest edit changes that function's core, so its data above; a changed signature regenerates its callers' heap certificates (data only). The bytecode offsets move for every function laid out after it, so their generated data that cites offsets (`Bundle`, frame walks, pairings, bridge checks) is regenerated too, with no hand proof involved. A hand proof changes only when the function's residual families or its B1 statement do; `regen-guest.sh`'s `changes` step lists the hand modules that read a changed function.
