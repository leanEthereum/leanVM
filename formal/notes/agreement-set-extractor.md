# The agreement-set knowledge extractor

The knowledge extractor (`LeanVM.transcriptExtractor := transcriptExtractorL`, `Protocol/KnowledgeSoundness.lean`) decodes uniquely, on agreement sets the transcript pins down, rather than list-decoding level 0's word. This note shows that it is correct with the opening's per-message errors and maps the proof. The idea is scaraven's (github.com/scaraven), from the lvm2 write-up's "advice decoding" annex.

## The extractor

Notation is Annex B's (`doc/leanvm/body/b-polynomial-commitment-scheme.tex`): levels `i < M`, fold rounds `j ≤ ℓ_i`, words `ω^(i,j)` (level `i`'s oracle folded by its first `j` challenges, `ω^(i,ℓ_i) = w^(i)`), degree `d_i = 2^κ_i − 1 + ℓ_i`, thresholds `A^(i,j) = A_i − (ℓ_i − j)`, high parts `φ^(i)`, final polynomial `P^fin`.

For a polynomial tuple `U` and a word `ω` let `S(U, ω)` be the set of columns where they agree. Define, for a level `i` and round `j ≥ 1`, the one-round step `Dec_{i,j}(U)`: decode the two halves of `ω^(i,j−1)` on the columns `S(U, ω^(i,j))` with at most one error, giving a tuple of degree at most `d_i` (or nothing). At a level boundary, `Up_i(U) := mpoly(g_U) + Ŵ_{κ_{i−1}} Σ_s φ^(i−1)_s X_s`, the polynomial of level `i−1`'s last round with the message of `U` as low part and the proof's high part. The extractor computes `T := P^fin`, then for `i = M−1` down to `0`, `j = ℓ_i` down to `1`, `T := Dec_{i,j}(T)`, and `T := Up_i(T)` between levels; the last `T` is a tuple of level 0, and its low parts are the stack, run as a candidate execution (`candidateExecutionIn`). Write `Back_{i,j}` for the composition of the remaining steps from round `(i,j)`, so the output is `Back_{M−1,ℓ}(P^fin)`.

Each step is one unique decoding of a lane with one error on a set of at least `A^(i,j)` columns, `2^(ℓ_i − j + 1)` lanes per round. Unique decoding with one error needs two tuples that each agree with the word on all but one column of `S` to agree on more than `d_i` columns, `|S| ≥ d_i + 3`; `|S| ≥ A^(i,j) ≥ A^(i,0) + 1` and `(A^(i,0))² > n_i d_i` give it at every admissible shape (a parameter check, decided over the table like the others).

## Correctness, with the opening's errors

Replace the opening's invariant (Annex B, proof of `thm:rbr`) by a **bad set** that is empty at the start:

`Bad_(i,j) := { U ∈ lst^(i,j) : U satisfies (W^(i,j), σ^(i,j)) and Back_(i,j)(U) is not a member of lst^(0,0) satisfying all input claims }`,

the invariant `(I'_(i,j))` being `Bad_(i,j) = ∅` (with the batching, OOD and query states as in Annex B). At the start of the opening `Back` is the identity and `Bad` is empty by definition. Each verifier message breaks the invariant with at most Annex B's probability, by the same events used forwards:

* **Fold `(α, β)` of round `(i,j)`.** Outside `B_1` (the MCA event of `thm:mca-affine` for the halves of `ω^(i,j−1)`, `excSet`), every `U ∈ lst^(i,j)` has a witness `(F, G)` with `U = G + (α + βX)F`, agreeing with the halves on all of `S(U, ω^(i,j))` but one column, so `(F, G) ∈ lst^(i,j−1)` and `Dec_{i,j}(U) = (F, G)` by uniqueness. Outside `B_2` (`lem:fold-list`: no pair of the list that violates the old claim satisfies the new one), if `U` satisfies the new claim then `(F, G)` satisfies the old one. So `U ∈ Bad_(i,j)` gives `(F, G) ∈ Bad_(i,j−1) = ∅`. Error `ε_aff + 2 jl(A^(i,j) − 1, d_i)/|E|`, as in Annex B.
* **Query message and `λ_(i+1)`.** By OOD separation at most one message `g*` of `lst^(i+1,0)` answers `y_(i+1)`. Outside the batching event, a `U ∈ Bad_(i+1,0)` satisfies all `J_(i+1)` claims, so `g_U = g*` and `Up_(i+1)(U)` matches `c_(i,q)` at every query. If it agrees with `w^(i)` on fewer than `A^(i,ℓ_i)` columns, every query landed in that set (probability `((A^(i,ℓ_i) − 1)/n_i)^(t_i)`, for the one `g*` fixed before the queries); otherwise `Up_(i+1)(U) ∈ lst^(i,ℓ_i)` satisfies the residual claim, so it is in `Bad_(i,ℓ_i) = ∅`. Error as in Annex B.
* **Final query message and tail.** If `P^fin` agrees with `w^(M−1)` on at least `A^(M−1,ℓ)` columns it is in the last list, and with the invariant `Back(P^fin)` satisfies the input claims unless `P^fin` violates the last claim, which the tail sumcheck catches (`2/|E|` a round). Otherwise the queries catch it.
* **Batching `λ_0`, OOD points.** Unchanged.

So, on an accepted proof outside the escape events, `C := Back(P^fin)` is a member of level 0's list satisfying every input claim of the opening, the commitment's OOD claim among them, so `C` is the one candidate the commitment's OOD answer leaves (`oodCands`). The front phases' doom is "`C*` is valid, or `C*` violates the current claims", which holds at the root unconditionally and escapes exactly when the phase's doom does (a case split: validity of `C*` is fixed once the OOD point is drawn). With it, `C` is a valid execution, and **the certificate's per-message errors are the opening's**. `romSound_log` applies unchanged to the verifier program that returns `verdict && !Good(history)`, `Good` the noncomputable statement "`Back(P^fin)` read off the history is valid", with these doom predicates, which hold at the root.

## Availability

The extractor reads only the adversary's queries; `romSound_log`'s extracted history reads the final cache, the verifier's queries included. The extractor needs every level's word and every fold challenge:

* **Every level's root**: a fresh hash answer of the verifier equal to one of the at most `rootCap` roots the replay reads has probability `rootCap · 2^-256` per verifier query (`prEvent_logged2_roots_le`).
* **Every fold challenge and the seed**: without failure, the verifier's permutations are first logged in the order it makes them (a permutation entry whose answer's capacity an input carries comes before it, `lt_of_cap`), so once one of them is past the adversary's log, so is every later one, the final query's proof of work's among them (`replay_chain`, `avail_or_hit`). That answer passes the grind, and its challenge names the final phase's first positions, at least `110` bits at every admissible shape (`finalBits_all`): a uniform answer does so with probability at most `2^-127`. The final proof of work's input is a function of the log (`grindData`) that persists as the log grows, so it is queried fresh at most once, and a potential that starts at `2^-127` and never rises bounds the hit by `2^-127` in all (`prEvent_logged2_hit_le`).

`lean_vm`'s error for the agreement-set extractor, `soundnessErrorAS`, adds these terms, and fits the end statement's bound (`soundnessRpmAS_le_statement`, `LeanVMSoundAS.lean`).

## In Lean

* **The opening** (`Protocol/Whir/Sound/`): the certificate with an acceptance predicate (`CertG`, `romSound_logG`, `romSound_of_impG`, `Spec.toCertG`), the invariant relativised (`TViol` with a predicate, `pullT` through near tuples at the round's threshold, `card_foldBadR_le`, `nextPred` across levels, `lvl0Pred` at level 0), the ghost opening (`whirLevelsG`, `verifyWhirG`, `verifyStackedOpeningG`) returning a `WhirTrace` with postcondition `traceGood`, and the descent over any near-tuple decoder (`Whir.Sound.Descent`: `descendTrace`, `descendTrace_of_traceGood`) at the deployed thresholds (`Whir.Params.DescentCheck`).
* **The assembly** (`EndToEnd/Sound/`): the ghost verifier (`leanVmProgramG`), `leanVmCert` a `CertG` with `GoodRun`, the end theorem through `doomed_of_not_extracted`; the cut ghost verifier at the final polynomial and its run (`Whir.Sound.Cut`, `CutRun`, `CutXrun`: `xcut_leanVmProgramG`).
* **The replay and availability** (`Compile.Rom.Replay`, `Compile.Rom.Chain`, `EndToEnd.Sound.Avail`, `AvailProb`): the cut verifier's replay on the adversary's permutation answers returns the ghost verifier's value at a state whose roots hold the history's commitments (`replayU_cut`), and the bounds above.
* **The extractor and its knowledge soundness**: over any near-tuple decoder (`EndToEnd.Sound.AgreementExtractor`: `extractAS`, `extractAS_valid`), in the random-oracle model (`LeanVMSoundAS`: `knowledgeSoundnessRomAS`) and the random-permutation model (`Rpm.ExtractF`, `LeanVMRpmAS`: the transcript's first forward answers extend the transfer's cached answers, `cacheF_firstForward`, and the extractor is monotone in its permutation answers, `extractAS_mono`).
* **The computable extractors**, each equal to the previous one: over values (`LowMsgC`, `DescentV`, `EndToEnd.Sound.ExtractorC`: `extractASC`), over the verifier's skeleton, which the announced layout and rate fix (`Skeleton`, `SkelLeaves`, `SkelBus`, `SkelFront`, `SkelWhir`, `SkelDecode`, `SkelExtract`: `skel_veBodyCut`, `dec_leanVmProgramCut`, `extractASF_of_extractASC`), and the cost machine's form: the transcript's first forward answers indexed (`Extraction.AS.Forward`), Keccak-256 through them (`Extraction.AS.Hash`), one-error Newton decoding on the first `d + 3` columns of the agreement set (`Whir.Sound.Newton`, `NewtonDecoder`, `NewtonK`), the descent over arrays with the next level's message by the additive NTT (`DescentA`, `DescentT`, `DescentC`, `StackA`), the replay on events' values (`SkelPairs`) and the lite layout (`Extraction.AS.Lite`: `extractL`, `transcriptExtractorL`). `LeanVM.knowledgeSoundness_holds` composes the equalities once (`Protocol/KnowledgeSoundness.lean`).
* **The price** (`Cost/AS/`): `extractL` compiled (`extractLC`, `Cost/AS/Top.lean`), every compiled function priced (`Cost/AS/Bounds/`: the replay, the seed, the skeleton and the decoders in `Replay.lean`, the descent in `Descent.lean` and `Trace.lean`, the step up, the stack and the words in `StepUp.lean`, the roots in `Roots.lean`, the finish and the top in `Finish.lean` and `Top.lean`: `extractL_cost`, wherever the extraction succeeds). The transcript's index of first forward answers is built once, and one preimage index serves every committed root (`leavesIn`, `preimageIn_transcriptIndex_mono`). The price is within the statement's budget (`Cost/AS/Bounds/Budget.lean`, `Cost/AS/Within.lean`: `asPrice_le`), and `compiledASExtractor` computes `transcriptExtractorL` within it (`Cost/AS/End.lean`: `asExtractor_output`).
