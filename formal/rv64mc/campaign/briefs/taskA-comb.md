## Why

The campaign to prove `main`'s total contract (`Final/MainSpecT.lean`) bottom-up needs a `HoldsT` for each of the ~229 functions `main` reaches. 94 of them already have a partial `Holds` proof with a functional postcondition (`notes/status.md`, class A). A total proof that only shows safety and termination (postcondition: what callers need for their own safety) is much cheaper than redoing the functional proof in total form. This phase proves that the two combine: the run is deterministic off the `ECALL`s, the total contract says it returns (or ends the run in `Stop`), and the partial contract says every return satisfies its postcondition, so the total contract holds with both postconditions.
