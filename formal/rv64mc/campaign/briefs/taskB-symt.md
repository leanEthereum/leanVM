## Why

The campaign to prove `main`'s total contract (`Final/MainSpecT.lean`) bottom-up needs a `HoldsT` (`Core/HoldsT.lean`) for each of the ~229 functions `main` reaches (~20,900 instructions, ~104 loops, no recursion). By hand with `TStep.lean`'s rules a function costs about 10 lines per instruction (`MemsetT.lean`: 359 lines for 38 instructions). `rv_sym` proves partial `Holds` contracts at a fraction of that, and 94 functions already have such proofs. A total-mode `rv_sym` that steps `rvIsa.Total p stmtCtx (WinOk p) Q s` goals with the same automation is the multiplier for the whole campaign, and lets the existing partial proof scripts be ported nearly mechanically. (A sibling phase proves that a safety-only `HoldsT` plus an existing `Holds` give the `Holds`'s postcondition in total form, so total proofs may use weak postconditions.)
