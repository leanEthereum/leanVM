### D1. `keccak_f`'s constant tables are one environment hypothesis, `DataRef.InData env (tables L)` (Proof/Hash/KeccakFT.lean `tables`, Table/HashT.lean `KeccakTables`)
- **Why**: the round constants (192 bytes) are copied by `memcpy` from the static data, and the `π` (200 bytes) and `ρ` (100 bytes, 13 words) tables are loaded in place. `CopyPreT` needs the source `inWindow` and `OffStack`; the `π` loop's store address `sp + 0x160 + 8 * π[j]` needs `π[j] < 25`. `InData` is the same kind of fact as `FieldT.ClmulTable` and `WhirT`'s tables.
- **Rejected**: (a) `DataRef.Words` only (gives values under `Static`, but no window or address range); (b) an environment hypothesis "the table ends below `stackLimit`" (an assumption about the layout, not a table content fact).
- **Claims**: `data_static` (no hypothesis beyond `Static m` and membership) proves every `env.data` word lies in `[staticLo, staticHi)`: if it did not, `Tactic.static_store` keeps `Static` after changing it, against `env.static_data`. `table_range` derives from it, `Windows.data_ok` and `env.layout`: the table is 8-aligned, `inWindow` for its whole length, and ends at or below `nextAddr` (so below `stackLimit - 8`, hence `OffStack`). `pi_lt` is `decide` over the 25 literal entries. The `ρ` table's entries are not needed (only shift amounts).
- **Evidence**: `#print axioms Rv64mc.Table.HashT.keccakFT` pinned to `[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]`; the theorem's only hypotheses are `env` and `hd : KeccakTables env`.
