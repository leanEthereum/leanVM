### D1. Every transcript-chain contract is safety only; none keeps its partial postcondition
- **Why**: the partial contracts of `absorb`, `sample`, `absorb_nonce`, `Transcript::new`, `next_scalar`, `grind_check` and `samples` (class A) state the sponge's lanes after `duplex`, i.e. `keccak_f`'s functional result. This is wave 3c's D7 decision for `duplex`, carried up the chain. `Sponge::new` and `hash_pair` are class C with the same dependency (`keccak_f`, `keccak_hash`).

### D2. The transcript's window reaches callers as a representation predicate, `TranscriptReprT.TranscriptT`, with ownership `OwnedT` separate
- **Definition** (`Proof/Transcript/TranscriptReprT.lean`): `TranscriptT env m a := SpongeT env m a ∧ InWin env a 360 ∧ Apart q 48 a 360 ∧ ProofT env m q` with `q := m.getMem (a + 328#64)` and `ProofT env m q := q % 8 = 0 ∧ InWin env q 48 ∧ NextT.StreamWin env m q`; `OwnedT env m top a := env.Owned m top a 360 ∧ env.Owned m top q 48`.
- **Why**: the brief asks to decide once: the window is an `InWin` conjunct inside the representation (the existing `NextT.PreT` already took `InWin` on the transcript, not its frame's bound). Ownership depends on the stack top, so it is kept apart: a callee's `PostT` gives `OwnedT` at the callee's entry stack pointer (`regs .x2`), and a caller re-derives its own by `OwnedT.of_next` (NEXT not lowered, proof address kept). The proof's address is written as `m.getMem (a + 328#64)`, not `proofAddr m a`, because the executor reads through stores only in that form (with `proofAddr`, `NextScalarT` left three callee-precondition goals the closer could not see through: recorded in the session, fixed by the change).
- **Rejected**: `∃ t, TranscriptAt env m a t` (wave 3c's plan, D6 there): `next`'s functional post gives no `TranscriptAt` on the `Err` path while its frame allows the stream offset written, so `next_scalar`'s post could not re-establish it on that path; and `ProofAt` carries the whole proof's contents, which safety does not read.
- **Claims**: `TranscriptT` reads exactly five words (`TranscriptT.of_get` takes five equalities and nothing else); `TranscriptT.frame`/`frameS`/`frameF` keep it across any frame whose `out` misses those words, given `OwnedT`; no index computed by the methods is unchecked (next: `geu` offset vs length before the element load; absorb: `ltu 3, n` before the slot store).
