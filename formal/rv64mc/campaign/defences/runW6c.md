### D1. `Vec<Form>::spec_extend` (202): `Proof/Layout/SpecExtendFormT.lean`, `SpecT`, `total`; `Table/FormT6c.specExtendForm`
- **Why**: the first deliverable. Safety-only (class D, no spec), with a postcondition shaped for its callers' loops: `199` calls it three times on one vector, `195` twice.
- **Pre (`PreT`)**: the vector `VecT` (24-byte elements), its header `StackObj` (at or above the stack pointer, below `0xc0000000`), its elements `FormsT`; their terms' blocks apart from the spare slots `[ptr + 24 len, ptr + 24 cap)` (`TermsApart`); `a1 <= a2`, `(a2 - a1) % 24 = 0`; **room**: `len + n <= cap`; the source, unless empty, `OffStack`, below `NEXT` or from `heapEnd`, apart from the spare slots; the source `FormsT` and its terms' blocks apart from the spare slots.
- **Post (`PostT`)**: `VecT` again; length `len + n`; capacity and pointer unchanged; `FormsT` of all `len + n` elements; `TermsApart` of all elements with the *new* spare slots (exactly the next call's `PreT` conjunct); the new elements' terms' blocks fresh (`CloneFormsFreshT.FreshTermsT` at `ptr + 24 len`). `Out` = the header's 24 bytes or the new slots (`SlotOut`). `Stop = AbortStop`.
- **Rejected**: (a) handling `reserve`'s growth: it would need the old elements moved through `Grown`'s bytes (as D2 does), and no call site grows: at both callers the buffer is allocated by the caller with the total count as capacity (199: the three slices' lengths' sum; 195: `1 + len`). The room conjunct holds at all five sites (argued in `notes/total-status.md`, Obligations, the new 199/195 entry). (b) `Owned` for the header instead of `StackObj`: with a heap header, the final length store could overlap an element's terms' block; both sites pass a frame slot (199: `sp + 8`; 195: `sp + 0x48`). (c) Apartness of the source from the header: not needed, since the frame is taken with `out := SlotOut` during the loop and `reserve`'s header writes are value-preserving (D1 claims).
- **Claims**: (1) during the loop the memory invariant is `CloneFormsT.MemOk` with `out = SlotOut regs m`, reused unchanged from `CloneFormsT` (same thirteen slots at the same offsets); `reserve`'s frame is composed in `total` because its `GrowOut` words are in `InRange a0 24` and the room case keeps their values (`hret1_17`). (2) Every loop has a measure: outer `Total.loop` on the `Form`s left (`OInv`), inner on the terms left (`IInv`). (3) The callee `Pre`s discharged: `reserve`'s `ReservePre` and `VecT` (through the prologue's stores), `alloc`'s, `Vec<usize>::clone`'s `ClonePreT` (the source term's monomial `BufT`, read through the frame from the entry memory, `ag`/`hBt` in `inner`), `handle_error` (aborts). (4) `TermsApart` concerns only the terms' blocks: the monomial buffers are only passed to `clone`, whose `Pre` asks `BufT` (no contents), so their overlap with the slots cannot break safety or `FormsT`.
- **Evidence**: build line above; `grep -n "TermsApart\|SlotOut" formal/rv64mc/Rv64mc/Proof/Layout/SpecExtendFormT.lean` shows the definitions and every use.
