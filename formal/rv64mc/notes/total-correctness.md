# Total correctness: `HaltsSafely`

`HaltsSafely image` (`Rv64mc/EndStatement.lean`): on every input of fewer than `2^16` words, the loaded image runs, every step taken by the statement's `step`, to the halting `ECALL` with `a0` in `{0, 1, 2}`. Its end theorem is `rv64_halts_of_main` (`Final/EndT.lean`), whose one hypothesis is `main`'s total contract `MainSpecT` (`Final/MainSpecT.lean`).

`BoolVerdict image` adds that `a0 = 2` only on an input that does not decode. Its end theorem is `rv64_boolVerdict` (`Final/Headline.lean`), from `main`'s partial contract `MainSpec` and its total contract `MainSpecB` (`Final/MainSpecB.lean`): `MainSpecT` with the stopping set `MainStopB words`, `a0 = 2` and `decodeInput words = none`. `MainSpecB` implies `MainSpecT`. Both end theorems are instances of `rv64_halts_with` (`Final/EndT.lean`), over `main`'s stopping set and the exit codes the run may end with, through `Start.totalWith` and `halts_of_main_with` (`Final/StartT.lean`).

What a proof of `MainSpecB` must discharge beyond `MainSpecT`: that no abort other than a malformed input's is reached on an input that decodes. `main`'s own aborts (`Input::word` or `Input::words` on an exhausted slice, input left over) match `decodeInput`'s failures (`readMany` on exhaustion, words left over), by reading `guest/src/main.rs` against `readInput`, not by proof. The others: the allocator out of heap (`AllocStop`), `collect`'s capacity overflow, and every panic on `verify_execution`'s path, among them `crates/verifier/src/whir.rs`'s `assert!` in `novel_basis` and `assert_eq!` in `fold_row`, `multilinear.rs`'s `eq_kernel` (a buffer of `1 << point.len()` entries), `field.rs`'s `Scaled::new` (a table per call, on an allocator that never frees), and every slice index, `unwrap` and `expect`. `collect` cannot overflow its capacity on an input of fewer than `2^16` words that decodes, since each item reads at least one word, so every count is below `2^16`. The abort study's runs of the verifier on generated and mutated proofs reached neither the allocator's abort nor a panic; they do not run `main`'s parser, and some exceed `2^16` words, so this is evidence, not proof.

## The pieces

* `Rv64mc/Total/` (namespace `Lprog`, on `lprog` alone): `Ascent.Total R accept G Q s`, the inductive "every run reaches an accepting state satisfying `Q`, and every state before it satisfies `G`, steps, and neither traps nor goes `wrong`"; `Isa.Total` at Lprog; one rule per instruction (`Total.label`, `opn`, `set`, `goto`, `cond`, `call`, `tail`, `ret`, `ext`, `ext_done`, `returned`); `Total.loop` (strong induction on a measure at a loop head); `Isa.TContract`, a total contract with a stopping set `Stop` (where the callee may end the run: at an accepting state with a nonempty stack) and the obligation `G` fixed, and `Total.call_contract`, `tail_contract`, `TContract.measure`.
* `Core/HoldsT.lean`: `stmtCtx` (every `ECALL` stops; every halt, `t0 = 0`, accepts), the obligation `WinOk` (a load or store lies in a window), `Windows` (where the windows lie: the stack top at most `0xc0000000`, `NEXT`'s address at least `0xa0000000` and 8-byte aligned, `NEXT` at least `0x10000000`, the heap's end at most `0x78000000`, the static data words in a window and 8-byte aligned, the code between the windows), `HoldsT` (`Holds`'s precondition plus `Windows`, its postcondition plus the code map kept, and `Stop`), `HoldsT.call`. `Final/EndT.lean` discharges `Windows`' facts about the image by kernel checks, and `_start` calls `main` at `stackTop`.
* `Final/HoldsTWitness.lean`: guards. `frameFn_total` (a function storing to and loading from its frame keeps a `HoldsT`, from `Windows` alone), `loadFn_next`, `loadFn_data` (a load of `NEXT` or of a static data word), `countdownFn_total` (a loop by `Total.loop`), `not_total_ecall`; `Total/Excludes.lean`: a state that only traps, only goes `wrong`, has no step or loops forever is total nowhere.
* `Final/Transfer.lean`: `halts_of_total`, from `Isa.Total` under machine-checked's `Sim` to `Halts` over `step`, reusing `Sim.split`, `back_instr` and `trap_instr`.
* `Proof/Runtime/HaltT.lean`, `PanicsT.lean`: `Aborts` (never returns, ends the run with `a0 = 2`) for `halt` and the panic chain, the pattern for a function that does not return.
* `Proof/Runtime/AllocT.lean`, `InputWordT.lean`, `MemsetT.lean`: total contracts of runtime functions that return or abort. `AllocSpecT`: the bump allocator returns `AllocSpec`'s block, or ends the run with `a0 = 2` exactly when no block of the size at the first multiple of 8 at or above `NEXT` ends within the heap (`AllocStop`). `InputWordSpecT`: `Input::word` returns `InputWordSpec`'s word, or ends the run with `a0 = 2` exactly when the reader's slice is empty (`WordStop`); its `WordPreT` puts the reader and, unless it is empty, its slice in windows, which `main`'s reader over the input satisfies (`wordPreT_input`). `MemsetSafeT`: `compiler_builtins`' `memset` and its C wrapper return `dst`, writing at most the doublewords of `[dst, dst + n)`, for a destination in a window (`InWin`); three loops by `Total.loop`. It does not state the bytes written, which is `MemsetSpec`'s partial `SetPost`.
* `Total/Combine.lean`, `Core/HoldsTOfHolds.lean`: a total contract and a partial one of the same function give a total contract with both postconditions (`TContract.and_contract`, generic, for a partial contract over a context that every step of the total one's is a step of; `HoldsT.of_holds`, `of_holds_code` and `of_holds_post` at `HoldsT` and `Holds`, with `stmtCtx` against `sp1Ctx` discharged). `Final/HoldsTOfHoldsUse.lean` uses it at the image for `Input::word`.
* `Tactic/TStep.lean`: the stepping rules and macros below.

## Proving a function total

A function with a partial proof (`Holds`, with its functional postcondition and memory frame) is proved total by a `HoldsT` of safety and termination, then `HoldsT.of_holds_post ht hp hpre`, which gives the `HoldsT` with the `Holds`'s postcondition, memory frame (`Out`), `AbiRegs` and `env.Static`. The `HoldsT` `ht` states as postcondition only what its callers need for their own total proofs (bounds on what it returns, say, or `True`) and may take the coarsest memory frame, `Out := fun _ _ _ => True`, under which the frame says only that `NEXT` grows within the heap, so it does not re-prove the write set. It still proves `AbiRegs`, `env.Static` and the code map kept, which every `HoldsT` postcondition includes. `HoldsT.of_holds_code ht hp hpre` spares even those: its `ht` is a bare `rvIsa.TContract` at `stmtCtx`, `WinOk p` and `HoldsT.pre` whose postcondition is only the code map kept, and the `Holds` gives `AbiRegs` and `env.Static` (`of_holds_post` is `of_holds_code` after dropping the `HoldsT`'s postcondition to the code map kept). `hpre` says the total contract's precondition implies the partial one's; the stopping set is the total contract's. `of_holds` keeps both postconditions and the total contract's memory frame. Neither needs the run to be deterministic. A function with no partial proof states its functional postcondition in the `HoldsT` itself.

What differs from a `Holds` proof, and so what `rv_sym` needs to step `rvIsa.Total p stmtCtx (WinOk p) Q s` goals:

* A rule set of its own: the `Total.*` rules in place of `SafeN.*`, with the same continuation shape. Each takes the obligation `WinOk p s` at the state it steps from: `WinOk.of_ne hp rfl nofun` at an instruction that is not an operation (a label, a branch, a call, a return, an `ECALL`), `WinOk.of_set hp rfl` at a register write, `WinOk.of_opn hp rfl trivial` at a `skip`, and `WinOk.of_opn hp rfl hw` at a load or store, where `hw : opWin op regs` is `inWindow addr width = true`, a new side goal about the address. Its facts come from `HoldsT`'s precondition: `Windows.stack` (between `stackLimit` and the stack top), `Windows.heap` (from `NEXT` to the heap's end), `Windows.nextAddr`, `Windows.data` and `dataSub` (a static data word, or a part of one). `Windows` covers what the function owns. A pointer it is passed (the input, a caller's stack object, a heap object allocated before the call) is in a window only by bounds the function's `Pre` (or its representation predicates) must state: `Windows.stackObj` takes `stackLimit ≤ ptr` and an end at most `0xc0000000`, `Windows.heapObj` takes `0x10000000 ≤ ptr` and an end at most the heap's end. Neither `Windows` nor `Holds`'s existing preconditions bound a heap pointer below, so each pointer argument of a total contract needs its window bounds and its alignment in `Pre`.
* A load or store must give a result: `Total.opn` takes `rvIsa.opn op regs m = some (r', m')`, so SP1's check is a goal, where `lsym` leaves it as a hypothesis `hm` because a fault is safe for `SafeN`. Its three parts: the address below `2^37` (from the window), aligned for its width (from the pointer's alignment in `Pre`, or `Windows.nextAddr_aligned` and `data_ok` for `NEXT` and the static data words: an access misaligned for its width anywhere in the image would make the function's `HoldsT` false), and, for a store, no code at it (`Windows.noCodeAt`, from the window). `frameFn_total` and `loadFn_total` show the pattern.
* After a call, `HoldsT.call` gives the code map kept and the frame (`NEXT` grows), from which `Windows.after` gives `Windows` again at the caller's stack top; `Windows.callee` lowers it to a callee's.
* A loop needs a measure that decreases on every iteration, not only an invariant: `Total.loop` at its head, the induction hypothesis applied where `rv_sym` stops at the head again. A recursive function: `TContract.measure` (or `Ascent.budget_induction` over a table).
* A call through `HoldsT.call` (or `Total.call_contract` with `h.contract`) has one more goal than `Holds.call`: `Q` at every halt in the callee's `Stop`. For a caller whose own `Stop` contains the callee's, it is the caller's `hstop`.
* A function that ends the run: `Total.ext_done` at the halting `Lext`, with `stmtCtx.accept` (`t0 = 0`) and `Q` there.

## Stepping total goals: `Tactic/TStep.lean`

`rv_sym` steps `SafeN` goals only. For `rvIsa.Total p stmtCtx (WinOk p) Q` goals, `TStep.lean` gives the `Total.*` rules with `WinOk` discharged in each (`TotalRv.label`, `set`, `goto`, `cond`, `ret`), and one rule per memory access the three proofs use with the operation's result given (`ld8`, `sd8`, `sb`), whose side goals are the address's window (`inWindow`), its alignment and, for a store, `noCodeAt`. SP1's range check follows from the window (`valid_of_inWindow`). The macros:

* `tstep hp`, `tsteps hp`: one step, or as many as apply, at a label, a register write or a jump, each followed by `tnorm`, which computes the new register value (unfolding `Expr.eval`, reading a register through the writes before it), so that the register file stays a chain of writes of values over the entry's rather than of expressions over earlier files, which grows with every step. `tsteps` passes a loop's head label too: to stop at a head, `iterate n tstep hp`.
* `tcond hp as h`: a branch into its two outcomes, `h` the comparison, after `tnorm at h`, over `toNat` for an unsigned comparison or one with zero.
* `tomega`: `tnorm`, then `omega`, for a side goal about numbers.

What is left by hand, and what an `rv_sym` for total goals would add: a load or store (`TotalRv.ld8 hp rfl hw hal ?_`, with `hw` from `Windows.stack`, `stackObj`, `heap`, `heapObj`, `nextAddr`, and `noCodeAt` from `Windows.noCodeAt` at the current memory, via `Windows.after`); a call (`HoldsT.call`, `Aborts.call`, with the callee found by `callee_site`); a return (`TotalRv.ret` and the continuation's postcondition); a loop (`Total.loop` with an invariant, entered at its head). A load reads memory as `getMem` of the address, not through earlier stores as `rv_sym` does: a proof that loads what it stored rewrites by `getMem_setMem` itself.
